# Toolpaths

The Toolpaths page, down the right, turns the selected vectors and the 3D model into
toolpaths, lists them, previews them cutting the material and saves them for the machine.
Pick a toolpath's tool to open its form; choose the cutter from the tool database there.

While a preview plays, its controls sit over the 2D and 3D views: Pause or Run, Single
Step, Run to Retract, Stop, the speed, and Progress to drag the tool on or back. At Speed
×1 the preview plays as fast as the machine would cut: each move at its tool's feed or
plunge rate, rapids at the Toolpath Summary's rapid rate. ×10 plays ten times faster, up
to ×1000. The line under the controls gives the machine time left, and how long that takes
to watch at the chosen speed. A finishing toolpath with a small bit can take hours, so
speed it up, drag Progress on, or press Stop (or Esc) to keep what is cut so far. Turn off
Animate Preview (the Toolpaths menu, Toolpath Drawing) to cut the whole preview at once,
with a progress window you can Cancel (or press Esc).
