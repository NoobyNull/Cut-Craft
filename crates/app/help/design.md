# Design

The Design page, down the left, draws and edits vectors, then models them in 3D. Its side
tabs: **Design** (these tools), **Sheets**, **Layers**, **Components** and **Library** (your own folders of models, pictures and drawings: double-click or drag one in, right-click for more).
Pick a tool to open its form on the page; **Apply** makes or changes the shape and
**Close** (or Esc) puts the tool away.
