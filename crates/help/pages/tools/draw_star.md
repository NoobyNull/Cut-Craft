## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_star/tour.gif)

1. Draw a star with as many points as you like.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_star/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Star**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_star/step-2.png)

3. Set the number of **Points** and how deep the points go (inner radius), then drag from the middle out, or type the sizes.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_star/step-3.png)
