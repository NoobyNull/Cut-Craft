## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/create_component_from_bitmap/tour.gif)

1. Turn a grey picture into a 3D relief: light parts high, dark parts low. Import the picture first.

   ![Step 1](pictures/create_component_from_bitmap/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Component from Bitmap**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/create_component_from_bitmap/step-2.png)

3. Set the height and press **Create**. Use [Edit Picture](help:edit_picture) first to adjust the tones.

   ![Step 3](pictures/create_component_from_bitmap/step-3.png)

4. The picture becomes a relief: the sky and sun stand high and the dark hills sit low.

   ![Step 4](pictures/create_component_from_bitmap/step-4.png)
