## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/vector_texture/tour.gif)

1. Fill a shape with rows of wavy lines or a decorative pattern, to carve as a texture. Select the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/vector_texture/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Create Vector Texture**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/vector_texture/step-2.png)

3. Choose the pattern and its spacing, waves and angle. Change the **Seed** for another random variation.

   ![Step 3](pictures/vector_texture/step-3.png)
