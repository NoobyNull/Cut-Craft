## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/nest_parts/tour.gif)

1. Nesting packs the selected shapes onto the board with as little waste as possible.

   ![Step 1](pictures/nest_parts/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Nest Parts**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/nest_parts/step-2.png)

3. Set the bit's width and the gap to leave, and the corner to start from, then press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/nest_parts/step-3.png)

**Bit diameter** is the width of the bit that cuts the parts out: the least gap left between them.
