## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/emboss/tour.gif)

1. Emboss makes a 3D part much lower while keeping its detail, for a shallow carving. Select the part.

   ![Step 1](pictures/emboss/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Emboss Component**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/emboss/step-2.png)

3. Set **Scale height**, the new height as a share of the old, and press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/emboss/step-3.png)
