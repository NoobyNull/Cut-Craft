## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/zoom_selected/tour.gif)

1. Zoom Selected fits what you selected in the view. Select it first.

   ![Step 1](pictures/zoom_selected/step-1.png)

2. Press **Zoom Selected** (under 2D View Control).

   ![Step 2](pictures/zoom_selected/step-2.png)

3. The view zooms to fit the selection.

   ![Step 3](pictures/zoom_selected/step-3.png)
