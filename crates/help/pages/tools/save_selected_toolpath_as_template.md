## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/save_selected_toolpath_as_template/tour.gif)

1. Save a toolpath you like as a template, to make the same cut on other jobs. Click it in the toolpath list to select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/save_selected_toolpath_as_template/step-1.png)

2. Press **Save Toolpath as Template** (under Templates) and name the file. It keeps the settings and the bits, not the shapes.

   ![Step 2](pictures/save_selected_toolpath_as_template/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- The template keeps the toolpath's group, and the groups holding that group, so loading it
  brings the same nesting back: see [Load Toolpath Template](help:load_template) and
  [Group Toolpaths](help:group_toolpaths).
