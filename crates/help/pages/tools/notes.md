## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/notes/tour.gif)

1. Notes keep free text with the job: the customer, the wood, anything to remember.

   ![Step 1](pictures/notes/step-1.png)

2. Press **Notes** (under File Operations).

   ![Step 2](pictures/notes/step-2.png)

3. Type the notes. Start them with a full stop (.) to have them open by themselves whenever the job is opened.

   ![Step 3](pictures/notes/step-3.png)
