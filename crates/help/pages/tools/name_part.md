## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/name_part/tour.gif)

1. Name a shape for the cut list and the labels. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/name_part/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Name Part**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/name_part/step-2.png)

3. Type the name and press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/name_part/step-3.png)
