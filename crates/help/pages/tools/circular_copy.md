## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/circular_copy/tour.gif)

1. Make copies spaced round a centre, like the spokes of a wheel. Select the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/circular_copy/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Circular Copy**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/circular_copy/step-2.png)

3. Set how many copies and the centre, and whether they turn as they go round.

   ![Step 3](pictures/circular_copy/step-3.png)
