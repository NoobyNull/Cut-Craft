**Celtic Weave Creator** (Gadgets menu) draws a Celtic knot: strands run diagonally across a grid
and bounce off its edges, crossing over and under each other. It draws the knot's loops as
smooth closed vectors, the rails [Extrude and Weave](help:extrude_and_weave) turns into a woven
3D model.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/celtic_weave/tour.gif)

1. On the **Gadgets** menu, choose **Celtic Weave Creator**. Set the knot grid's **Columns**, **Rows** and cell size.

   ![Step 1](pictures/celtic_weave/step-1.png)

2. Tick **Create cross-section** for a half-round profile beside the knot, ready for Extrude and Weave.

   ![Step 2](pictures/celtic_weave/step-2.png)

3. Press **Create**. The knot's loops are drawn as smooth closed vectors, the rails Extrude and Weave turns into a weave.

   ![Step 3](pictures/celtic_weave/step-3.png)

## Settings

- **Columns** and **Rows**: how many cells the knot grid has across and up.
- **Cell size**: the size of each square cell. Bigger cells make a bigger knot.
- **Cuts**: crossings where the strands turn back rather than cross. A vertical cut turns them
  back to the side they came from, a horizontal one up or down. Cuts make the knot's pattern.
- **Create cross-section**: adds a half-round profile beside the knot, ready to pick as the
  cross-section in Extrude and Weave.
