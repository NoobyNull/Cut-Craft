In a double-sided job, **Copy to Other Side** copies the selected vectors onto the other side of
the material, mirrored as the board is turned over, so they line up through it. Use it for a cut
out that is cut half from each side, or holes that meet in the middle.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/copy_to_other_side/tour.gif)

1. In a double-sided job, select the vectors to cut from the other side as well.

   ![Step 1](pictures/copy_to_other_side/step-1.png)

2. Right-click them and choose **Copy To ▸ Other Side**. **Swap Sides** shows the copy there, mirrored so it lines up through the board.

   ![Step 2](pictures/copy_to_other_side/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- It is greyed out unless the job is double-sided: set that in [Job Setup](help:job_setup).
- See [Swap Sides](help:swap_sides) to work on the other side.
