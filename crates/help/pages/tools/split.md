## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/split/tour.gif)

1. Split cuts a 3D part in two with a shape, like a cookie cutter. Select the part and the closed shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/split/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Split Component**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/split/step-2.png)

3. Press **Apply**: the inside and the outside become two parts.

   ![Step 3](pictures/split/step-3.png)
