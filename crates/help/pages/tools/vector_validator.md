## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/vector_validator/tour.gif)

1. Vector Validator finds shapes that would stop a toolpath: lines crossing themselves, doubled shapes, zero-length spans.

   ![Step 1](pictures/vector_validator/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Vector Validator**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/vector_validator/step-2.png)

3. It selects the faulty shapes so you can fix them, and can fix zero-length spans for you.

   ![Step 3](pictures/vector_validator/step-3.png)
