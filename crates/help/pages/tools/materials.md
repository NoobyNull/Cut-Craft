## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/materials/tour.gif)

1. Materials shows the job in the wood (or plastic or metal) you will cut. On the Toolpaths page, press **Materials** (under Job and Tools).

   ![Step 1](pictures/materials/step-1.png)

2. Pick the **Material**. Woods show their grain in the 3D view; **Custom** is a plain colour of your own.

   ![Step 2](pictures/materials/step-2.png)

3. Choose how the preview colours what the toolpaths cut (**Machined area**): the material's own colour, one colour, or each toolpath's colour. Then press **OK**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/materials/step-3.png)

4. The 3D view and the toolpath previews now show the job in that material.

   ![Step 4](pictures/materials/step-4.png)
