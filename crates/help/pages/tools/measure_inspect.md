## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/measure_inspect/tour.gif)

1. Measure distances and angles, and read a shape's size.

   ![Step 1](pictures/measure_inspect/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Measure / Inspect**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/measure_inspect/step-2.png)

3. Click two points to measure between them, or click a shape to see its size.

   ![Step 3](pictures/measure_inspect/step-3.png)
