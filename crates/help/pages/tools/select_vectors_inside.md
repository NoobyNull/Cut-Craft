**Select Vectors Inside** selects every vector inside the selected closed shape: the holes in a
part, or the letters on a plaque. Right-click ▸ **Select Vectors Inside**, or Edit ▸ Selection.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/select_vectors_inside/tour.gif)

1. Select a closed shape with other vectors inside it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/select_vectors_inside/step-1.png)

2. Right-click and choose **Select Vectors Inside**. Everything inside the shape is selected in its place.

   ![Step 2](pictures/select_vectors_inside/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Use it to pick a part's holes for a drilling or pocket toolpath.
