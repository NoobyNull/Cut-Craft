## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/set_size/tour.gif)

1. Make the selection an exact size. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/set_size/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Set Size**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/set_size/step-2.png)

3. Type the width or height; keep **Link** ticked to keep its proportions. Then press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/set_size/step-3.png)
