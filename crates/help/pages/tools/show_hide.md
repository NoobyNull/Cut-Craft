**Show** and **Hide** on the right-click menu of a layer, toolpath or component show or hide it,
or a whole set at once. Hidden layers' vectors are not drawn or picked, hidden toolpaths are left
out of Preview Visible and of saving the shown ones, and hidden components are left out of the model.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/show_hide/tour.gif)

1. Right-click a layer in **Layers** (or a toolpath or component in its list) and choose **Show** or **Hide**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/show_hide/step-1.png)

2. **Show ▸ Only This** hides every other one, so you see just this layer's vectors. **Show ▸ All** brings them all back.

   ![Step 2](pictures/show_hide/step-2.png)

## The menus

- **Show ▸ This** and **Hide ▸ This**: the one you right-clicked.
- **Show ▸ Only This**: shows it and hides all the others.
- **Show ▸ All But This**: shows all the others and hides it.
- **Show ▸ All** and **Hide ▸ All**: every one of them.

**Undo** takes a change back. In the Layers list, the eye before a layer's name does the same for
one layer.
