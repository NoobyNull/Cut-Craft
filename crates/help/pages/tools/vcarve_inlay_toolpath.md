## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. A V-carve inlay is two carvings that fit together: a pocket in one board and a plug from another, turned over and glued in. Select the shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **V-Carve Inlay** (under Edges and Inlays).

   ![Step 2](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Pocket Depth**: the deepest the pocket goes.

   ![Step 3](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Set the **Glue Gap**: the space left under the plug for glue once it is pressed home.

   ![Step 4](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. You get two toolpaths: the pocket, and the plug carved round a mirrored copy.

   ![Step 5](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths** to check both halves.

   ![Step 6](pictures/vcarve_inlay_toolpath/step-6.png)

Cut the plug from a separate board of the contrasting wood. When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
