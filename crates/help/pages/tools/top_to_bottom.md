## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/top_to_bottom/tour.gif)

1. Space the selected shapes out evenly from top to bottom.

   ![Step 1](pictures/top_to_bottom/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Space Top to Bottom**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/top_to_bottom/step-2.png)

3. The gaps between them are now even.

   ![Step 3](pictures/top_to_bottom/step-3.png)
