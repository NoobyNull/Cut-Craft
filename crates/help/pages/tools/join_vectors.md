## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/join_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Join open lines whose ends nearly meet into one line, or one closed shape. Select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/join_vectors/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Join Vectors**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/join_vectors/step-2.png)

3. Set how close the ends must be to join.

   ![Step 3](pictures/join_vectors/step-3.png)
