## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/slice/tour.gif)

1. Slice cuts a model too thick for your board into layers, each carved from its own board and glued up.

   ![Step 1](pictures/slice/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Slice Model**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/slice/step-2.png)

3. Set each slice's thickness and press **Apply**: each slice becomes its own part.

   ![Step 3](pictures/slice/step-3.png)

4. The model is split into slices, each its own part to carve from its own board.

   ![Step 4](pictures/slice/step-4.png)
