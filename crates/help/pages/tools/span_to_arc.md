**Convert Span to Arc** turns the span you right-click into a round arc. Drag the arc's middle to
make it bulge more or less. It is on node editing's right-click menu.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/span_to_arc/tour.gif)

1. Select the shape and press **Node Edit**, then right-click the span to bend.

   ![Step 1](pictures/span_to_arc/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Convert Span to Arc**. The side is a round arc now: drag its middle to make it bulge more or less.

   ![Step 2](pictures/span_to_arc/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- See [Convert Span to Bezier](help:span_to_bezier) for a free curve, and
  [Convert Span to Line](help:span_to_line) to straighten it again.
