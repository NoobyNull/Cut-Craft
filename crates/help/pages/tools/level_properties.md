**Level Properties** changes a level of the 3D model: its name, how it combines with the levels
below, whether it is shown, its base height, its mirror mode and, in a rotary job, wrapping.
Right-click the level in **Components** ▸ **Properties**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/level_properties/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components**, right-click a level's name and choose **Properties**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/level_properties/step-1.png)

2. Rename it, pick how it combines with the levels below, and raise it with a **Base height**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/level_properties/step-2.png)

3. **Mirror Mode** copies the level's model across the middle, so you model one half or quarter.

   ![Step 3](pictures/level_properties/step-3.png)

## Settings

- **Name**: what it is called in the Components list.
- **Combine Mode**: how the level meets the levels below it.
- **Visible**: hidden levels are left out of the model.
- **Base Height**: raises the whole level on a flat plane this thick.
- **Mirror Mode**: copies the level's model across the middle (left to right, top to bottom, or
  into each quarter), so you model one half or quarter and get the rest.
- **Wrapping** (rotary jobs): what runs off one side comes back on the other.
