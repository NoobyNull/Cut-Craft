**Unselect All** (Edit ▸ Selection) clears the selection, so no tool acts on anything. Pressing
Esc, or clicking an empty part of the view, does the same.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/unselect_all/tour.gif)

1. Selected vectors are drawn dashed, and a tool acts on them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/unselect_all/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Edit ▸ Selection ▸ Unselect All** (or press Esc, or click an empty spot). Nothing is selected now.

   ![Step 2](pictures/unselect_all/step-2.png)
