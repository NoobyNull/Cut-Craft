Double-click a group to step inside it and change one thing in it. See [Groups](help:groups).

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/group/tour.gif)

1. Grouping joins shapes so they select and move as one. Select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/group/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Group**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/group/step-2.png)

3. They now move together. Ungroup takes them apart again.

   ![Step 3](pictures/group/step-3.png)
