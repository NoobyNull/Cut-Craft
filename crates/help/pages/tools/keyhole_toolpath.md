## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Keyhole slots let a sign hang flat on screws. On the back of the sign, draw a small circle where each slot starts and select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Keyhole** (under Holes).

   ![Step 2](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Choose which way the slot runs from the hole (**Direction**). For a sign hung on the wall, the slot runs up, so the screw head slides to its top.

   ![Step 3](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Set the **Slot Length** and the **Slot Depth**: deep enough that the cutter's wide part is below the surface.

   ![Step 4](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. The keyhole cutter plunges at each circle and runs along the slot.

   ![Step 5](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. The new lines show each slot running from its entry hole.

   ![Step 6](pictures/keyhole_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
