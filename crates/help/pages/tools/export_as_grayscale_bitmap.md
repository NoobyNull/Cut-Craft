## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/export_as_grayscale_bitmap/tour.gif)

1. Save the 3D model as a grey picture: light is high, dark is low.

   ![Step 1](pictures/export_as_grayscale_bitmap/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Export Model as Grayscale Picture** and name the file.

   ![Step 2](pictures/export_as_grayscale_bitmap/step-2.png)
