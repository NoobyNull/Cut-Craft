**Move to Layer** puts the selected vectors on another layer, to show, hide or cut them with that
layer's other vectors. Right-click the selection ▸ **Move to Layer**, then the layer.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/move_to_layer/tour.gif)

1. Select the vectors to move to another layer.

   ![Step 1](pictures/move_to_layer/step-1.png)

2. Right-click them and choose **Move to Layer**, then the layer. In **Layers**, right-click a layer ▸ **Move Selection Here** does the same.

   ![Step 2](pictures/move_to_layer/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- In **Layers**, right-click a layer ▸ **Move Selection Here** does the same.
- See [Add Layer](help:add_layer) to make a new one first.
