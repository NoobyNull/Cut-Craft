## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/left_to_right/tour.gif)

1. Space the selected shapes out evenly from left to right.

   ![Step 1](pictures/left_to_right/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Space Left to Right**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/left_to_right/step-2.png)

3. The gaps between them are now even.

   ![Step 3](pictures/left_to_right/step-3.png)
