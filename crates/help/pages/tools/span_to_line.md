**Convert Span to Line** turns the curved span you right-click into a straight line between its
two nodes. It is on node editing's right-click menu.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/span_to_line/tour.gif)

1. Select a curved shape and press **Node Edit**, then right-click the curved span to straighten.

   ![Step 1](pictures/span_to_line/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Convert Span to Line**. That span is a straight line between its two nodes now.

   ![Step 2](pictures/span_to_line/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- See [Convert Span to Arc](help:span_to_arc) and
  [Convert Span to Bezier](help:span_to_bezier) to curve it again.
