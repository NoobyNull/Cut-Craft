**Delete** under the Toolpath List removes the picked toolpath from the job. The vectors it was
made from stay, and **Undo** brings it back.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Click the toolpath to remove in the Toolpath List.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. Press **Delete** above the list (or the Delete key). The vectors it was made from stay.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. It is gone from the list. **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) brings it back.

   ![Step 3](pictures/delete_toolpath/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- Pressing the Delete key just after clicking a toolpath in the list does the same.
- To delete several at once, right-click one ▸ **Delete** ▸ **Visible**, **Invisible** or
  **All**: see [Delete Items](help:delete_items).
- To keep a toolpath but not cut it, untick it in the list instead.
