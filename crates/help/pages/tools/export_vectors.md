## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/export_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Send your drawing to another program, as DXF, SVG or EPS. Select what to send, or nothing for everything.

   ![Step 1](pictures/export_vectors/step-1.png)

2. Press **Export Vectors** (under File Operations), choose the type and name the file.

   ![Step 2](pictures/export_vectors/step-2.png)
