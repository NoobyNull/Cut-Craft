## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/two_rail_sweep/tour.gif)

1. Two Rail Sweep stretches a cross-section between two rails all the way along, as for a curved handle or a boat hull. Select the two rails, then the cross-section.

   ![Step 1](pictures/two_rail_sweep/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Two Rail Sweep**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/two_rail_sweep/step-2.png)

3. Choose how the cross-section is scaled along the way, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/two_rail_sweep/step-3.png)

4. The cross-section is swept along the rails into a 3D part.

   ![Step 4](pictures/two_rail_sweep/step-4.png)
