## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/embellish/tour.gif)

1. Embellish adds folk-style swirls and ornament designed to fit the shape. Select a closed shape for a fill, or an open line to run along.

   ![Step 1](pictures/embellish/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Embellish**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/embellish/step-2.png)

3. Pick a style and its settings. Warnings appear in red at the foot of the form.
   If a setting can't be made (a raise height of 0, say), the ornament you had stays as
   it was and the form says why, in red: fix the setting and it is made again in its place.

   ![Step 3](pictures/embellish/step-3.png)
