**Rename** on a toolpath group's right-click menu gives the group a new name in the Toolpath
List.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/rename_toolpath_group/tour.gif)

1. In the Toolpath List, right-click a group's name and choose **Rename**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/rename_toolpath_group/step-1.png)

2. Type a name that says what the group cuts, such as Sign or Box sides.

   ![Step 2](pictures/rename_toolpath_group/step-2.png)

3. Press **Apply**. The group has its new name in the list.

   ![Step 3](pictures/rename_toolpath_group/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- Name a group for what it cuts, so the list reads like your plan.
- Groups inside it stay inside it under its new name.
- See [Group Toolpaths](help:group_toolpaths).
