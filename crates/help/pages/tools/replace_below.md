## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/replace_below/tour.gif)

1. Replace Below removes or flattens the parts of a 3D part lower than a height. Select the part.

   ![Step 1](pictures/replace_below/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Replace Below**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/replace_below/step-2.png)

3. Set the height, choose remove or flatten, then press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/replace_below/step-3.png)

4. The lower part of the shape is cleared, leaving only what stands above the height.

   ![Step 4](pictures/replace_below/step-4.png)
