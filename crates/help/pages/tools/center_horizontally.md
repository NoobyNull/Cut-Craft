## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/center_horizontally/tour.gif)

1. Line up the middles of the selected shapes, left to right.

   ![Step 1](pictures/center_horizontally/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Centre Horizontally**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/center_horizontally/step-2.png)

3. Their middles now line up up and down the job.

   ![Step 3](pictures/center_horizontally/step-3.png)
