## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/paste_in_place/tour.gif)

1. Paste in Place puts what you copied exactly where it was, rather than where the pointer is: handy for copying between sheets or jobs. Copy something first (Ctrl+C).

   ![Step 1](pictures/paste_in_place/step-1.png)

2. Press **Paste in Place** (under File Operations).

   ![Step 2](pictures/paste_in_place/step-2.png)

3. The copy lands on top of where the original was, ready to move or change.

   ![Step 3](pictures/paste_in_place/step-3.png)
