## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/rounding_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Rounding turns a square or round blank into a smooth cylinder on a rotary axis. Press **Setup Material** and pick **Rotary**, with the finished diameter.

   ![Step 1](pictures/rounding_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Rounding** (under 3D Toolpaths).

   ![Step 2](pictures/rounding_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Say what you start from (**Blank**: square or round) and its **Blank size**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/rounding_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Choose the **Method**: round the cylinder as it turns, or along it in rows.

   ![Step 4](pictures/rounding_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate** to make the toolpath.

   ![Step 5](pictures/rounding_toolpath/step-5.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
