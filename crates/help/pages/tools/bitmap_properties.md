**Bitmap Properties** sets a picture's name and exactly where it sits on the job and how big it
is. Use it to line a traced or modelled picture up with your drawing.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/bitmap_properties/tour.gif)

1. Click the picture to select it, then right-click it and choose **Bitmap Properties**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/bitmap_properties/step-1.png)

2. Type where its corner goes (**X**, **Y**) and how big it is (**Width**, **Height**).

   ![Step 2](pictures/bitmap_properties/step-2.png)

3. Press **Apply**. The picture moves and resizes; the picture itself stays as it was.

   ![Step 3](pictures/bitmap_properties/step-3.png)

To move a picture roughly, you don't need this form: drag the picture in the 2D view.

## Settings

- **Name**: what the picture is called.
- **X** and **Y**: where the picture's left and bottom edges sit.
- **Width** and **Height**: its size on the job.

To fade the picture in the 2D view, use **Appearance** on the 3D view's right-click menu. See
[Import Vectors / Bitmap](help:import_vectors) for bringing a picture in.
