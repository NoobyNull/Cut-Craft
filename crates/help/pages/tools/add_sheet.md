**Add New Sheet** adds another sheet of material to the job, the same size as the sheet it was
made from. Use sheets when the parts of one project are cut from more than one board, or don't
all fit on one.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/add_sheet/tour.gif)

1. Open **Sheets** at the top of the Design page and press **Add New Sheet...** under the list.

   ![Step 1](pictures/add_sheet/step-1.png)

2. Give the sheet a name. It starts the same size as the sheet it was made from.

   ![Step 2](pictures/add_sheet/step-2.png)

3. Press **Create**. The new sheet is listed; click it to work on it, and change its size in **Job Setup**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/add_sheet/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- Right-click a sheet ▸ **Insert New Sheet** makes one the size of that sheet.
- The new sheet is not made active: click it to work on it, and set its size and thickness in
  [Job Setup](help:job_setup) while it is active.
- See [Activate Sheet](help:activate_sheet) and [Copy / Move to Sheet](help:send_to_sheet).
