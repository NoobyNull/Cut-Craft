## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/align_top/tour.gif)

1. Line up the tops of the selected shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/align_top/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Align Top**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/align_top/step-2.png)

3. Their tops now line up.

   ![Step 3](pictures/align_top/step-3.png)
