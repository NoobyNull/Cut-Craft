**Select Layer Vectors** on a layer's right-click menu selects every vector on that layer, and
nothing else, to move, edit or cut them together.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/select_layer_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Open **Layers** and right-click the layer whose vectors you want.

   ![Step 1](pictures/select_layer_vectors/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Select Layer Vectors**. Every vector on that layer is selected, and nothing else.

   ![Step 2](pictures/select_layer_vectors/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- **Edit ▸ Selection ▸ Select All Vectors On Current Layer** does the same for the current
  layer.
