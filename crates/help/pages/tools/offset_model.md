## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/offset_model/tour.gif)

1. Offset Model grows or shrinks the whole visible model by a distance, all round.

   ![Step 1](pictures/offset_model/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Offset Model**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/offset_model/step-2.png)

3. Set the distance (negative shrinks it) and press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/offset_model/step-3.png)

4. The whole model has grown outwards by the distance.

   ![Step 4](pictures/offset_model/step-4.png)
