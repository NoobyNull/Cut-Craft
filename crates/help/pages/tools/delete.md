**Delete** removes the selected vectors, text and pictures from the job. Press the Delete key,
or choose **Edit ▸ Delete**. **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) brings them back.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete/tour.gif)

1. Select what you want to remove: vectors, text or pictures.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete/step-1.png)

2. Press **Delete** on the keyboard (or **Edit ▸ Delete**). Changed your mind? **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) brings it back.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- In node editing with nodes picked, Delete removes those nodes, not the shape.
- Just after you click a toolpath in the list, Delete removes that toolpath instead.
- Vectors on locked layers can't be selected, so they are never deleted by mistake.
