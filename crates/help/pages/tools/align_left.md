## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/align_left/tour.gif)

1. Line up the left edges of the selected shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/align_left/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Align Left**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/align_left/step-2.png)

3. Their left edges now line up.

   ![Step 3](pictures/align_left/step-3.png)
