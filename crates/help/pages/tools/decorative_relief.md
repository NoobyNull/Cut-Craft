## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/decorative_relief/tour.gif)

1. Decorative Relief fills a shape with raised scrolls and leaves, ready to carve. Select the closed shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/decorative_relief/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Decorative Relief**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/decorative_relief/step-2.png)

3. Pick the style and its height, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/decorative_relief/step-3.png)

4. Raised scrolls and leaves fill the rectangle, ready to carve.

   ![Step 4](pictures/decorative_relief/step-4.png)

Raising the pieces is spread over the processor's cores, as many as
[Options ▸ Performance](help:performance) allows, so your other programs stay smooth while
it works.
