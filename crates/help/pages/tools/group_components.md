**Group** puts two or more selected 3D components on one level into a group. The group's
components combine with each other first, then the group combines with the rest of the model
as one.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/group_components/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** and pick two or more components on one level (Shift-click to add one).

   ![Step 1](pictures/group_components/step-1.png)

2. Right-click one and choose **Group**, then give the group a name.

   ![Step 2](pictures/group_components/step-2.png)

3. Press **Apply**. They are one group in the list: they combine with each other first, then with the rest.

   ![Step 3](pictures/group_components/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- Shift-click components in the Components list to pick more than one.
- Change the group's name and combine mode in [Group Properties](help:group_properties), and take
  it apart with [Ungroup](help:ungroup_components).
