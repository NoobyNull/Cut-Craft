## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. A laser can burn a photo or picture: darker parts get more power. Import the picture (drop it on the sheet) and click it to select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Laser Picture** (under Laser).

   ![Step 2](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Power** for black. Light parts get less.

   ![Step 3](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Tick **Dither image** for materials that only burn or don't (such as some plastics): the laser switches fully on and off in tiny dots to give the greys.

   ![Step 4](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Set the **Line spacing**: closer lines give more detail but take longer. Then press **Calculate**.

   ![Step 5](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. The toolpath follows the picture: more power where it is darker. It joins the toolpath list, ready to save for the laser.

   ![Step 6](pictures/laser_picture_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right, [save it for your laser](help:save_toolpaths).
