## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_curve/tour.gif)

1. Draw a smooth curve that passes through every point you click.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_curve/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Curve**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_curve/step-2.png)

3. Click the points in the view. The curve bends smoothly through each one; press Esc to finish.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_curve/step-3.png)
