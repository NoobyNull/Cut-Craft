**Move Level Up** and **Move Level Down** on a level's right-click menu move it one place in the
Components list, with its components. Levels combine from the bottom up, so the order changes the
model.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/reorder_level/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** and right-click the level to move: levels combine from the bottom up.

   ![Step 1](pictures/reorder_level/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Move Level Down** (or Up). Its components go with it, and the model is built again in the new order.

   ![Step 2](pictures/reorder_level/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- See [Level Properties](help:level_properties) for how a level combines.
