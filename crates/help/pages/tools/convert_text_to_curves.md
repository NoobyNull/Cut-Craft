## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/convert_text_to_curves/tour.gif)

1. Turn text into plain shapes, so you can change the letters' outlines with node editing. Select the text.

   ![Step 1](pictures/convert_text_to_curves/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Convert Text to Curves**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/convert_text_to_curves/step-2.png)

3. The letters are now shapes. They can no longer be edited as text.

   ![Step 3](pictures/convert_text_to_curves/step-3.png)
