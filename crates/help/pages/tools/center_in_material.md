## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/center_in_material/tour.gif)

1. Put the selection in the middle of the board. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/center_in_material/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Centre in Material** (under Align Objects).

   ![Step 2](pictures/center_in_material/step-2.png)

3. It moves to the middle of the material.

   ![Step 3](pictures/center_in_material/step-3.png)
