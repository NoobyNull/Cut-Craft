The tick beside a toolpath group's name in the Toolpath List shows or hides every toolpath in
the group at once, the toolpaths in its own groups too. Hidden toolpaths are left out of the
preview and out of saving the visible toolpaths.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/show_toolpath_group/tour.gif)

1. The tick beside a group's name shows or hides every toolpath in it, its groups' toolpaths too.

   ![Step 1](pictures/show_toolpath_group/step-1.png)

2. Untick it to hide them all from the preview and from saving visible toolpaths. A dash in the tick means only some are hidden.

   ![Step 2](pictures/show_toolpath_group/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Ticked: every toolpath in the group shows. Empty: none do. A dash: some do. Clicking a
  dash shows them all.
- One **Undo** takes the whole group's tick back.
- Each toolpath still has its own tick: see [Toolpath Properties](help:toolpath_properties).
- An empty group has no toolpaths to show, so its tick is greyed out.
