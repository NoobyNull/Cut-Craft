## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/array_copy/tour.gif)

1. Make a grid of copies of the selection. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/array_copy/step-1.png)

2. Under **Arrange**, press **Array Copy** (under Array Objects).

   ![Step 2](pictures/array_copy/step-2.png)

3. Set how many rows and columns and the gaps between, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/array_copy/step-3.png)
