Cut Craft can arrange its window four ways. Every tool is in every layout: only where the
buttons sit changes. Cut Craft starts in **Ribbon**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/interface_layout/tour.gif)

1. Pick how the window is arranged: Ribbon, Rail, Canvas or Classic.

   ![Step 1](pictures/interface_layout/step-1.png)

2. Press **Interface Layout** (under 2D View Control).

   ![Step 2](pictures/interface_layout/step-2.png)

3. Click the layout you want, then press **Use This Layout**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/interface_layout/step-3.png)

The window changes at once and Cut Craft remembers your choice. **Cancel** leaves it as it
was. **View ▸ Window Layout** and **Edit ▸ Options ▸ General ▸ Window layout** pick a layout
too.

## The four layouts

**[Ribbon](help:layout_ribbon)**: tabs of tools across the top (Design, Model, Toolpaths),
your sheets, layers and toolpaths on the left, and the open tool's settings on the right.

![The Ribbon layout, light theme on the left and dark on the right](pictures/layouts/ribbon-light-and-dark.png)

**[Rail](help:layout_rail)**: a column of tool groups down the left. Click one to list its
tools by name. The open tool's settings are on the right.

![The Rail layout, light theme on the left and dark on the right](pictures/layouts/rail-light-and-dark.png)

**[Canvas](help:layout_canvas)**: the whole window is your drawing. A Modeling bar of
tool groups on the left, a Toolpaths bar on the right, the open tool's settings and your sheets, layers and toolpaths float over it, and a
box in the header finds a tool by name.

![The Canvas layout, light theme on the left and dark on the right](pictures/layouts/canvas-light-and-dark.png)

**[Classic](help:layout_classic)**: the Design page of tool icons on the left, the Toolpaths
page on the right, and tabs down the sides.

![The Classic layout, light theme on the left and dark on the right](pictures/layouts/classic-light-and-dark.png)

## When Cut Craft asks

First-time setup asks which layout you want, with Ribbon picked. If you used Cut Craft
before this question existed, it asks once after you update. **Keep** leaves your window as
it was.

Before the layout changes, Cut Craft [backs up your settings](help:backups), so the layout and
favourites you had are kept.

Not sure where a tool went? Press **Ctrl+K** for [Find Tool](help:find_tool) and type part
of its name. Your [favourites](help:favourites) are the same in every layout.
