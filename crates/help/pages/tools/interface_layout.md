## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/interface_layout/tour.gif)

1. Choose how the panels are laid out, and save layouts of your own.

   ![Step 1](pictures/interface_layout/step-1.png)

2. Press **Interface Layout** (under 2D View Control).

   ![Step 2](pictures/interface_layout/step-2.png)

3. Pick a layout, or save the one you have.

   ![Step 3](pictures/interface_layout/step-3.png)
