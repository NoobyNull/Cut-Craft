## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/rotate/tour.gif)

1. Turn the selection by an exact angle. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/rotate/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Rotate**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/rotate/step-2.png)

3. Type the **Angle** and the point to turn about, then press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/rotate/step-3.png)
