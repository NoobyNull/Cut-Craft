## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_freehand/tour.gif)

1. Draw by hand, like a pencil: the line follows the pointer and is smoothed.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_freehand/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Freehand**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_freehand/step-2.png)

3. Hold the button down and draw in the view.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_freehand/step-3.png)
