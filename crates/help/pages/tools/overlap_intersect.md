## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/overlap_intersect/tour.gif)

1. Overlap keeps only the part where the selected shapes overlap. Select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/overlap_intersect/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Overlap**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/overlap_intersect/step-2.png)

3. Only the shared part is left.

   ![Step 3](pictures/overlap_intersect/step-3.png)
