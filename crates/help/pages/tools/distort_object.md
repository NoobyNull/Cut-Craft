## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/distort_object/tour.gif)

1. Bend the selection into a new shape by moving the corners of its box, or along a curve. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/distort_object/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Distort Object**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/distort_object/step-2.png)

3. Drag the corner handles to bend it.

   ![Step 3](pictures/distort_object/step-3.png)
