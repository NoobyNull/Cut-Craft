## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/weld_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Weld joins overlapping shapes into one outline. Select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/weld_vectors/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Weld Vectors**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/weld_vectors/step-2.png)

3. One outline goes round both; the lines inside are gone.

   ![Step 3](pictures/weld_vectors/step-3.png)
