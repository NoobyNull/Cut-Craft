## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/join_move_end_points/tour.gif)

1. Join the selected open lines by moving their ends together.

   ![Step 1](pictures/join_move_end_points/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Join By Moving End Points**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/join_move_end_points/step-2.png)

3. The nearest ends are moved to meet.

   ![Step 3](pictures/join_move_end_points/step-3.png)
