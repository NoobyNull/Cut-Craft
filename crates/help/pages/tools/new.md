## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/new/tour.gif)

1. Start a new, blank job, made with your Job Defaults (Edit ▸ Options ▸ Job Defaults).

   ![Step 1](pictures/new/step-1.png)

2. Press **New** (under File Operations, at the top of the Design page). A blank sheet opens: change its size with [Setup Material](help:setup_material).

   ![Step 2](pictures/new/step-2.png)

## What the blank job starts with

Its size, kind, Z zero, X0 Y0, rapid heights and home all come from **Edit ▸ Options ▸ Job
Defaults** ([Changing settings](help:settings)). Change them there once for your machine, and
every new job starts that way. The job that is open is never changed by them.

Nothing to save yet: a blank job you have not drawn on closes without asking, so opening a
file over it, or pressing New again, just replaces it.

If you would rather fill in every setting before a job opens, as Cut Craft used to, tick
**Classic Startup Flow** on the Job Defaults page. Cut Craft then starts on its start page,
and New opens [Job Setup](help:job_setup) first.
