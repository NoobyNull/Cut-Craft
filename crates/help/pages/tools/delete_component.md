**Delete** on a component's right-click menu removes that 3D component from the model.
**Undo** brings it back.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete_component/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** at the top of the Design page and right-click the component to remove.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete_component/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Delete**. It is gone from the model; **Undo** brings it back.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete_component/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- To leave a component out without losing it, hide it: right-click ▸ **Hide ▸ This**.
- The vectors it was made from stay, so you can make it again.
