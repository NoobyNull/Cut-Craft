## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/create_shape_round/tour.gif)

1. Create Shape raises a closed shape into a 3D form. Draw the shape and select it: here a circle.

   ![Step 1](pictures/create_shape_round/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, open **Model** and press **Create Shape: Round** (under Shape Creation).

   ![Step 2](pictures/create_shape_round/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Angle** the sides rise at and a **Height** limit if you want a flat top, then press **Create**. The circle becomes a dome.

   ![Step 3](pictures/create_shape_round/step-3.png)

4. The circle rises into a dome, shown here in the 3D view.

   ![Step 4](pictures/create_shape_round/step-4.png)
