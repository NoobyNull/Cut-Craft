## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/select/tour.gif)

1. Press **Select** (or Esc) to pick shapes. Most tools work on the shapes you have selected.

   ![Step 1](pictures/select/step-1.png)

2. Click a shape's line to select it. Handles appear round it: drag them to move, size or turn it.

   ![Step 2](pictures/select/step-2.png)

3. Hold **Shift** and click another shape to add it. Dragging a box round shapes selects them all; click empty space to select nothing.

   ![Step 3](pictures/select/step-3.png)

To move, size or turn what you selected exactly, use [Transform](help:transform).
