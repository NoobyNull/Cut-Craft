## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/center_horizontally_in_material/tour.gif)

1. Centre the selection left to right on the board.

   ![Step 1](pictures/center_horizontally_in_material/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Centre Horizontally in Material**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/center_horizontally_in_material/step-2.png)

3. It moves to the board's middle, left to right.

   ![Step 3](pictures/center_horizontally_in_material/step-3.png)
