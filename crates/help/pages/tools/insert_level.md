**Insert New Level** adds an empty level on top of the 3D model's levels. Levels combine from
the bottom up, so a level on top can add to or cut into everything under it.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/insert_level/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** at the top of the Design page and right-click a level's name.

   ![Step 1](pictures/insert_level/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Insert New Level** and give the level a name.

   ![Step 2](pictures/insert_level/step-2.png)

3. Press **Create**. The new level goes on top: it combines with the levels below it.

   ![Step 3](pictures/insert_level/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- Put components on it with [Move to Level](help:move_to_level).
- Change how the level combines in [Level Properties](help:level_properties).
