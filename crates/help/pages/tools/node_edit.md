## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/node_edit/tour.gif)

1. Node editing moves the points (nodes) a shape is made of, to reshape it. Select the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/node_edit/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Node Edit**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/node_edit/step-2.png)

3. Drag a node to move it. Right-click a node or a span for more: add or delete nodes, make a span straight or curved.

   ![Step 3](pictures/node_edit/step-3.png)
