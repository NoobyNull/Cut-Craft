**Select All Open Vectors** (Edit ▸ Selection) selects every vector whose ends do not meet.
Open vectors can't be pocketed or cut out inside or outside, so find them before making
toolpaths.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/select_all_open_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Open vectors, whose ends do not meet, can't be pocketed or cut out inside or outside.

   ![Step 1](pictures/select_all_open_vectors/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Edit ▸ Selection ▸ Select All Open Vectors** to find them all, then join or close them.

   ![Step 2](pictures/select_all_open_vectors/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Close them with [Join / Close Vectors](help:join_close_vectors) or
  [Join Open Ends](help:join_open_ends).
- **View ▸ Show Open Vector Ends** marks their ends on screen.
