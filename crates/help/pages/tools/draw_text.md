## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_text/tour.gif)

1. On the Design page, press **Draw Text**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_text/step-1.png)

2. Click on the sheet where the words start, then type them in the **Text** box.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_text/step-2.png)

3. Pick a **Font** and set the **Height** of the capital letters. The words on the sheet change as you go.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_text/step-3.png)

Lettering is usually carved with [V-Carve](help:vcarve_engrave_toolpath).
