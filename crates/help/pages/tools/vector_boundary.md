## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/vector_boundary/tour.gif)

1. Draw one outline round several shapes, for a border or a backing board. Select the shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/vector_boundary/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Vector Boundary**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/vector_boundary/step-2.png)

3. Choose how close the outline follows them, and how far out it sits.

   ![Step 3](pictures/vector_boundary/step-3.png)
