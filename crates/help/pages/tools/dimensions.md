## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/dimensions/tour.gif)

1. Dimensions draw measurements on the job: lengths, angles and radii, with arrows and the number.

   ![Step 1](pictures/dimensions/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Dimensions**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/dimensions/step-2.png)

3. Choose the kind (length, horizontal, vertical, angle, radius), then click the points to measure.

   ![Step 3](pictures/dimensions/step-3.png)
