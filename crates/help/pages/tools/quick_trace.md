## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/quick_trace/tour.gif)

1. Quick Trace turns a picture into shapes in one click, with settings chosen to suit it. Select the picture.

   ![Step 1](pictures/quick_trace/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Quick Trace**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/quick_trace/step-2.png)

3. The shapes are drawn over the picture. For more control, use [Trace Bitmap](help:trace_bitmap).

   ![Step 3](pictures/quick_trace/step-3.png)
