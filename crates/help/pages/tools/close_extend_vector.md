## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/close_extend_vector/tour.gif)

1. Close an open line into a shape, so it can be pocketed or cut round. Select the open lines.

   ![Step 1](pictures/close_extend_vector/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Close / Extend Vector**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/close_extend_vector/step-2.png)

3. Choose to close it with a straight line from end to start, or by running its end lines on until they meet.

   ![Step 3](pictures/close_extend_vector/step-3.png)
