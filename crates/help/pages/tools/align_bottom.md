## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/align_bottom/tour.gif)

1. Line up the bottoms of the selected shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/align_bottom/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Align Bottom**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/align_bottom/step-2.png)

3. Their bottoms now line up.

   ![Step 3](pictures/align_bottom/step-3.png)
