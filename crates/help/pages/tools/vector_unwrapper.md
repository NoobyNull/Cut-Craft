## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/vector_unwrapper/tour.gif)

1. On a rotary job, Vector Unwrapper turns a cross-section of the finished shape into the profile Two Rail Sweep needs. Select the cross-section.

   ![Step 1](pictures/vector_unwrapper/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Vector Unwrapper**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/vector_unwrapper/step-2.png)

3. Pick the centre to unwrap round, then press **Apply**. Use the profile with [Two Rail Sweep](help:two_rail_sweep).

   ![Step 3](pictures/vector_unwrapper/step-3.png)
