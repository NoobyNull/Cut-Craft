## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/trim_objects/tour.gif)

1. Trim the selected shapes to the shape you pick last: what is outside it is cut away. Select them, the trimming shape last.

   ![Step 1](pictures/trim_objects/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Trim Objects**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/trim_objects/step-2.png)

3. Only the parts inside the last shape are kept.

   ![Step 3](pictures/trim_objects/step-3.png)
