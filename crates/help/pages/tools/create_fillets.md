## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/create_fillets/tour.gif)

1. Round the sharp corners of a shape, or cut dog-bones into inside corners so a round bit can reach them. Select the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/create_fillets/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Create Fillets**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/create_fillets/step-2.png)

3. Choose a rounded corner or a dog-bone, set the radius, then click the corners (or do every corner at once).

   ![Step 3](pictures/create_fillets/step-3.png)
