## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/save_all_visible_toolpaths_as_template/tour.gif)

1. Save every toolpath shown in the list as one template: a whole job's cuts, such as a sign's lettering, pocket and cut-out, ready for the next sign. Untick any you do not want in it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/save_all_visible_toolpaths_as_template/step-1.png)

2. Press **Save All Shown Toolpaths as Template** (under Templates) and name the file. Use **Load Toolpath Template** to bring them into another job.

   ![Step 2](pictures/save_all_visible_toolpaths_as_template/step-2.png)
