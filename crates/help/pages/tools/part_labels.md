## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/part_labels/tour.gif)

1. Part labels are stickers for the parts you cut out, one for each named part. Open the **Output** tab on the Toolpaths page and press **Print Part Labels** (under Paperwork for the Job).

   ![Step 1](pictures/part_labels/step-1.png)

2. Choose what goes on each label and the label sheet, then print.

   ![Step 2](pictures/part_labels/step-2.png)
