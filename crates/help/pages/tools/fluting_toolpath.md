## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/fluting_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Flutes are grooves that taper in and out, as on a column. Draw a line for each flute and select them. Each flute starts at its line's first point.

   ![Step 1](pictures/fluting_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Fluting** (under Carve and Engrave).

   ![Step 2](pictures/fluting_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Flute Depth**: how deep the groove is at its deepest.

   ![Step 3](pictures/fluting_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Choose the **Flute Type**: taper down over the whole line, taper at the start only, or taper in at the start and out at the end.

   ![Step 4](pictures/fluting_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. A ball nose bit gives rounded flutes, a V-bit sharp ones.

   ![Step 5](pictures/fluting_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths**: each flute is shallow at its ends and deepest in the middle.

   ![Step 6](pictures/fluting_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
