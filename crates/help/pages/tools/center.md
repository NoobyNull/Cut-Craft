## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/center/tour.gif)

1. Centre the selected shapes on each other.

   ![Step 1](pictures/center/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Centre**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/center/step-2.png)

3. Their middles now line up.

   ![Step 3](pictures/center/step-3.png)
