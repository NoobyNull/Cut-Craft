## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/import_component_3d_model/tour.gif)

1. Bring in a 3D model (STL or OBJ) or saved 3D clipart to carve.

   ![Step 1](pictures/import_component_3d_model/step-1.png)

2. Under **Import**, press **Import 3D Model** and pick the file, then size and place it.

   ![Step 2](pictures/import_component_3d_model/step-2.png)
