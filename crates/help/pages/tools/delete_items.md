**Delete** on the right-click menu of a layer, toolpath or component deletes it or a whole set
at once: **This** (the one you right-clicked), **Visible** (every shown one), **Invisible** (every
hidden one), **Empty** (layers with nothing on them) or **All**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete_items/tour.gif)

1. Open **Layers** at the top of the Design page and right-click a layer, then choose **Delete**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete_items/step-1.png)

2. Pick **This**, **Visible**, **Invisible** or **Empty**. Empty clears away every layer with nothing on it.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete_items/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- A job always keeps one layer: if every layer would go, the current one is kept, and the form
  says so.
- Deleting a layer deletes the vectors on it. **Undo** brings it all back in one step.
