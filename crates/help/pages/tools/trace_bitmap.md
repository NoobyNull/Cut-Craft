## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/trace_bitmap/tour.gif)

1. Trace turns a picture into shapes you can cut. Import the picture and select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/trace_bitmap/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Trace Bitmap**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/trace_bitmap/step-2.png)

3. Choose black and white or colour, and how many colours. The preview shows the shapes; press **Apply** to keep them.

   ![Step 3](pictures/trace_bitmap/step-3.png)
