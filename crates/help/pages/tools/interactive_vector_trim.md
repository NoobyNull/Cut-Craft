## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/interactive_vector_trim/tour.gif)

1. Trim away pieces of lines between where they cross other lines, by clicking them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/interactive_vector_trim/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Interactive Vector Trim**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/interactive_vector_trim/step-2.png)

3. Click a piece to cut it away up to the nearest crossings, or drag across several.

   ![Step 3](pictures/interactive_vector_trim/step-3.png)
