## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/auto_layout_text/tour.gif)

1. Fit text to fill a shape as big as it will go, wrapping it onto lines. Select the text and the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/auto_layout_text/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Auto Layout Text**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/auto_layout_text/step-2.png)

3. Set the border to leave round the edge. The words are sized and wrapped to fill the shape.

   ![Step 3](pictures/auto_layout_text/step-3.png)
