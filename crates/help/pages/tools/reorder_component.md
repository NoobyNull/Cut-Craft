**Move Up** and **Move Down** over the Components list move the picked component one place among
its level's components. The list combines from the bottom up, so the order changes the model.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/reorder_component/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** and pick a component: the list combines from the bottom up.

   ![Step 1](pictures/reorder_component/step-1.png)

2. Press **Move Up** or **Move Down** over the list to move it one place among its level's components.

   ![Step 2](pictures/reorder_component/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- A component higher in the list is combined later: a Subtract component on top cuts into the
  ones under it.
- To move it to another level, use [Move to Level](help:move_to_level).
