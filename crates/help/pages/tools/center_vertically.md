## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/center_vertically/tour.gif)

1. Line up the middles of the selected shapes, front to back.

   ![Step 1](pictures/center_vertically/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Centre Vertically**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/center_vertically/step-2.png)

3. Their middles now line up across the job.

   ![Step 3](pictures/center_vertically/step-3.png)
