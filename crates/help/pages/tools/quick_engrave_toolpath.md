## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Quick Engrave marks shapes or lettering at a shallow depth. Select what to engrave.

   ![Step 1](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Quick Engrave** (under Carve and Engrave).

   ![Step 2](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Choose **Outline** to run along the lines, or **Fill** to mark the whole inside with rings or hatching.

   ![Step 3](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Set the **Depth**: a shallow mark, often half a millimetre.

   ![Step 4](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate** to make the toolpath.

   ![Step 5](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. With Fill, the lines step in from the outline until the star is covered.

   ![Step 6](pictures/quick_engrave_toolpath/step-6.png)

For deeper lettering with crisp corners, use [V-Carve](help:vcarve_engrave_toolpath). When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
