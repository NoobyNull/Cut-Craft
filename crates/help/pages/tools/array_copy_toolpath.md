## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Array Copy repeats a toolpath in a grid, to cut several of the same part from one board. Make the toolpath for one part first: here a Profile round a circle.

   ![Step 1](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Array Copy** (under From Other Toolpaths).

   ![Step 2](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set how many **Rows** and **Columns** of copies, counting the first.

   ![Step 3](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Set the distances between copies across (**X**) and up (**Y**) the job.

   ![Step 4](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. The copies follow the first: change and recalculate it, and they change too.

   ![Step 5](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Six parts from one toolpath.

   ![Step 6](pictures/array_copy_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
