## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/bottom_to_top/tour.gif)

1. Space the selected shapes out evenly from bottom to top.

   ![Step 1](pictures/bottom_to_top/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Space Bottom to Top**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/bottom_to_top/step-2.png)

3. The gaps between them are now even.

   ![Step 3](pictures/bottom_to_top/step-3.png)
