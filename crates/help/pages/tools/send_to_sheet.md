**Copy Selected Vectors Here** and **Move Selected Vectors Here** on a sheet's right-click menu
send the selected vectors onto that sheet. Each keeps its layer and group. Moving takes them off
this sheet; copying leaves them here too.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/send_to_sheet/tour.gif)

1. Select the vectors, then open **Sheets** and right-click the sheet they should go on.

   ![Step 1](pictures/send_to_sheet/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Copy Selected Vectors Here** or **Move Selected Vectors Here**. They keep their layers and groups there.

   ![Step 2](pictures/send_to_sheet/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Both are greyed out on the active sheet, or with nothing selected.
- See [Add New Sheet](help:add_sheet).
