## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/crop_bitmap/tour.gif)

1. Trim a picture to a shape. Select the picture and the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/crop_bitmap/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Crop Bitmap**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/crop_bitmap/step-2.png)

3. Everything outside the shape is cleared to white.

   ![Step 3](pictures/crop_bitmap/step-3.png)
