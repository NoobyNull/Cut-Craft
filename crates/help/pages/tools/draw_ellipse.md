## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_ellipse/tour.gif)

1. Draw an oval: a stretched circle, for plaques and frames.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_ellipse/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Ellipse**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_ellipse/step-2.png)

3. Drag from the middle out to a corner of the box it fits in, or type the centre, width and height in the form.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_ellipse/step-3.png)
