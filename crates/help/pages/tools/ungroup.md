**Ungroup** takes the selected groups apart one level. Each piece goes back onto the layer it is
on (grouping never moves vectors between layers). A group or text that was grouped comes back out
as it was, and text stays editable. Ungrouping text itself turns it into curves.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/ungroup/tour.gif)

1. Select a group: a click picks all of it, as one.

   ![Step 1](pictures/ungroup/step-1.png)

2. Right-click and choose **Ungroup ▸ Ungroup onto Original Object Layers**. Each piece can be picked on its own again.

   ![Step 2](pictures/ungroup/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- To change one thing in a group without taking it apart, double-click the group to step inside
  it. See [Groups](help:groups).
- To put every piece on the current layer, use
  [Ungroup onto Current Layer](help:ungroup_onto_current_layer).
