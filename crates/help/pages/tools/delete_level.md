**Delete Level** removes a level of the 3D model and the components on it. Right-click the level
in **Components**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete_level/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** at the top of the Design page and right-click the level to remove.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete_level/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Delete Level**. The level and the components on it are gone; a job always keeps one level.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete_level/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- A job always keeps one level, so it is greyed out on the last one.
- To keep its components, move them first with [Move to Level](help:move_to_level).
