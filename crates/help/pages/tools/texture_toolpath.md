## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/texture_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Texture covers an area with shallow scallops of random depth and length, like a hand-tooled finish. Select the area's outline.

   ![Step 1](pictures/texture_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Texture** (under Carve and Engrave).

   ![Step 2](pictures/texture_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Max Cut Depth** and **Max Cut Length**: the deepest and longest any scallop can be. Each is shorter and shallower at random.

   ![Step 3](pictures/texture_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Set the **Stepover** (the gap between rows) and the rows' **Angle**.

   ![Step 4](pictures/texture_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. Change the **Seed** for another random pattern.

   ![Step 5](pictures/texture_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths** to see the tooled surface.

   ![Step 6](pictures/texture_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
