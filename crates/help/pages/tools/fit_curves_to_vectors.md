## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/fit_curves_to_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Fit Curves redraws rough shapes (from a trace or a scan) as smooth arcs and curves. Select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/fit_curves_to_vectors/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Fit Curves to Vectors**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/fit_curves_to_vectors/step-2.png)

3. Choose arcs, curves or straight lines and how closely they follow the shape.

   ![Step 3](pictures/fit_curves_to_vectors/step-3.png)
