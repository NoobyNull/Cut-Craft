## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/subtract_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Subtract cuts the other shapes out of the biggest, like a cookie cutter. Select them all.

   ![Step 1](pictures/subtract_vectors/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Subtract Vectors**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/subtract_vectors/step-2.png)

3. The biggest shape now has a bite taken out where the others overlapped it.

   ![Step 3](pictures/subtract_vectors/step-3.png)
