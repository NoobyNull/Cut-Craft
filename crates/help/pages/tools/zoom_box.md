## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/zoom_box/tour.gif)

1. Zoom Box zooms in on the part you draw a box round.

   ![Step 1](pictures/zoom_box/step-1.png)

2. Press **Zoom Box** (under 2D View Control).

   ![Step 2](pictures/zoom_box/step-2.png)

3. Drag a box round the part to see close up.

   ![Step 3](pictures/zoom_box/step-3.png)
