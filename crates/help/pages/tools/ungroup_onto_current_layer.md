**Ungroup onto Current Layer** takes the selected groups apart one level, like
[Ungroup](help:ungroup), and puts every piece on the current layer, whatever layer it was on.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/ungroup_onto_current_layer/tour.gif)

1. Click the layer the pieces should go on in **Layers**, then select the group.

   ![Step 1](pictures/ungroup_onto_current_layer/step-1.png)

2. Right-click and choose **Ungroup ▸ Ungroup onto Current Layer**. The pieces come apart, all on that layer.

   ![Step 2](pictures/ungroup_onto_current_layer/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Double-click a group to step inside it and change one thing in it. See [Groups](help:groups).
