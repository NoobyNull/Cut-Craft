## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/mirror/tour.gif)

1. Flip the selection, as in a mirror. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/mirror/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Mirror**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/mirror/step-2.png)

3. Choose which way to flip, and whether to keep the original, then press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/mirror/step-3.png)
