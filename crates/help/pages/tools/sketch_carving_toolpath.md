## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Sketch carving carves the lines of a picture, like a pencil sketch: wherever it is darker than around it. Import the picture and select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Sketch Carving** (under Carve and Engrave).

   ![Step 2](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. On the **Sketch** tab, choose the **Source**: the picture, or the 3D model's dips and edges.

   ![Step 3](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. On the **Sketch** tab, set the **Line thickness**: heavier lines, but less detail, as it goes up.

   ![Step 4](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**, then **Preview All Toolpaths** to see the sketch.

   ![Step 5](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. The sketch's lines carved into the board.

   ![Step 6](pictures/sketch_carving_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).

The **Bit** box on the form says which bit cuts it: **Select…** picks another from the [Bit Library](help:tool_database), and **Edit…** changes its sizes, feeds and speeds there.
