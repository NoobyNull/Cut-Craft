## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/import_vectors_bitmap/tour.gif)

1. Bring in a drawing (DXF, SVG) or a picture (PNG, JPG and more) to cut, trace or draw over. You can also just drop the file on the sheet.

   ![Step 1](pictures/import_vectors_bitmap/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, open **Import** and press **Import Vectors or Picture**, then pick the file.

   ![Step 2](pictures/import_vectors_bitmap/step-2.png)
