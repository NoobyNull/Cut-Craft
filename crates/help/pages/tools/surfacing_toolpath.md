## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Surfacing flattens the top of the board, or your spoilboard, with a wide bit. Nothing needs to be selected to flatten the whole board. On the Toolpaths page, press **Surfacing** (under Cut Out and Clear).

   ![Step 1](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. Choose what to flatten: the **whole material**, the **spoilboard** on your machine's bed, or the inside of the shapes you selected.

   ![Step 2](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Depth to Take Off** the top. A light skim of a millimetre or so is usually enough to make it flat.

   ![Step 3](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Pick the **Pattern**: rows along X, rows along Y, or a spiral from the outside in. The bit always starts beside the board, never plunging into it.

   ![Step 4](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. The rows run past every edge so the edges come out clean.

   ![Step 5](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths** to see the whole top skimmed flat.

   ![Step 6](pictures/surfacing_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
