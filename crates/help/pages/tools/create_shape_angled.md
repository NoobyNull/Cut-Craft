## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/create_shape_angled/tour.gif)

1. An angled shape rises in straight slopes, like a roof or a pyramid. Select the closed shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/create_shape_angled/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Shape: Angled**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/create_shape_angled/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Angle** of the slopes, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/create_shape_angled/step-3.png)

4. The circle rises into a cone with straight sloping sides.

   ![Step 4](pictures/create_shape_angled/step-4.png)
