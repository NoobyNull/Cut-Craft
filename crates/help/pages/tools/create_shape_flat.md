## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/create_shape_flat/tour.gif)

1. A flat shape is a raised plateau with straight sides, for lettering or a base. Select the closed shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/create_shape_flat/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Shape: Flat**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/create_shape_flat/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Height** of the plateau, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/create_shape_flat/step-3.png)

4. The circle becomes a flat plateau with straight sides.

   ![Step 4](pictures/create_shape_flat/step-4.png)
