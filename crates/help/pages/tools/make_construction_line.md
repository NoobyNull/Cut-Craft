## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/make_construction_line/tour.gif)

1. Select the vectors that only place things, such as a centre line or a curve to bend along.

   ![Step 1](pictures/make_construction_line/step-1.png)

2. Right-click and choose **Make Construction Line**. It is drawn dashed, and is never cut or saved for the machine.

   ![Step 2](pictures/make_construction_line/step-2.png)

Read [Construction lines](help:construction_lines) for everything they do, and how to hide
them with **View ▸ Show Construction Lines**.
