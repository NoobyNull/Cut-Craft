## Steps

1. Select the vectors that should only place things, not be cut.
2. Right-click one of them and pick **Make Construction Line**.
3. They turn into soft blue dash-dot lines. Placing tools still follow them, but no toolpath
   will ever cut them and no exported file includes them.

Read [Construction lines](help:construction_lines) for everything they do, and how to hide
them with **View ▸ Show Construction Lines**.
