**Customize Toolbars** puts every tool and every one of your bars in one window, so you can
build a toolbar without hunting for each tool's right-click menu. Open it from **View ▸
Customize Toolbars...**, or right-click the dots at the start of any bar and pick
**Customize Toolbars...**.

## The window

- **Left: every tool**, under its group's name. Type in **Find a tool** to narrow the list.
  Click a tool, or click a group's name to pick the **whole group**.
- **Right: your toolbars.** [Favourites](help:favourites) comes first, then the toolbars
  you made. Click one to see what is on it, in order. **Main tools** is listed greyed out:
  it holds every tool, so it cannot be changed.

## The buttons

1. **Add →** puts the tool or group picked on the left at the end of the toolbar picked on
   the right.
2. **← Remove** takes the item picked on the right off its toolbar.
3. **Move Up** and **Move Down** move it along. On Favourites, Create and Edit tools stay
   ahead of toolpath tools.
4. **New Toolbar** makes a toolbar holding the tool or group picked on the left.
5. **Rename** names the toolbar picked on the right; **Delete** deletes it. Favourites can
   be renamed but never deleted.

A button that can do nothing yet is greyed out: pick something first. Every change shows on
the toolbar at once, and is kept with your other settings. See
[Toolbars of Your Own](help:toolbars) for moving, snapping and locking a toolbar.
