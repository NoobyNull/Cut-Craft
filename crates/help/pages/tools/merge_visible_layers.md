**Merge Visible** moves every vector on a shown layer onto the layer you right-click, then
removes the shown layers left empty. Hidden layers stay as they are, and so do locked ones.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/merge_visible_layers/tour.gif)

1. Open **Layers**, hide any layer to keep apart, then right-click the layer to merge into.

   ![Step 1](pictures/merge_visible_layers/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Merge Visible**. Every shown layer's vectors move onto it, and the emptied layers go.

   ![Step 2](pictures/merge_visible_layers/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Hide a layer first to keep it apart.
- A hidden layer merged into is shown, and a locked one unlocked, so the work stays in sight.
- Layers cut from another material are named in red, as their vectors now take this layer's.
