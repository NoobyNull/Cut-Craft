## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/text_on_a_curve/tour.gif)

1. Lay words along a curve, as on a badge or a sign's arch. Select the text, then Shift-click the curve.

   ![Step 1](pictures/text_on_a_curve/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Text on a Curve**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/text_on_a_curve/step-2.png)

3. Choose whether the letters sit above, on or below the curve, and whether they turn with it. The text stays editable.

   ![Step 3](pictures/text_on_a_curve/step-3.png)
