## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/plate_production/tour.gif)

1. Make many name plates or badges from one design and a list of names. Select the design.

   ![Step 1](pictures/plate_production/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Plate Production**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/plate_production/step-2.png)

3. Pick the data file; text with !!name!! in it is filled in from each row.

   ![Step 3](pictures/plate_production/step-3.png)
