## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/center_vertically_in_material/tour.gif)

1. Centre the selection front to back on the board.

   ![Step 1](pictures/center_vertically_in_material/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Centre Vertically in Material**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/center_vertically_in_material/step-2.png)

3. It moves to the board's middle, front to back.

   ![Step 3](pictures/center_vertically_in_material/step-3.png)
