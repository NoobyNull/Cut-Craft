## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/create_component_from_visible_model/tour.gif)

1. Make one new 3D part from everything visible, keeping the parts it came from hidden.

   ![Step 1](pictures/create_component_from_visible_model/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Component from Visible Model**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/create_component_from_visible_model/step-2.png)

3. A new part holding the model joins the Components list; the others are hidden.

   ![Step 3](pictures/create_component_from_visible_model/step-3.png)
