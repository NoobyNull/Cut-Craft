A toolpath's name, whether it is shown, and its colour in the preview are changed from the
Toolpath List: right-click ▸ **Rename**, the tick before it, and its coloured square.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/toolpath_properties/tour.gif)

1. Right-click a toolpath in the Toolpath List and choose **Rename**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/toolpath_properties/step-1.png)

2. Type a name that says what it cuts, such as Cut out, and press **Apply**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/toolpath_properties/step-2.png)

3. The tick before a toolpath shows or hides it, and its coloured square sets its colour in the preview.

   ![Step 3](pictures/toolpath_properties/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- **Preview Color: No Fill** on the right-click menu shows its areas in the material's own
  colour.
- Hidden (unticked) toolpaths are not drawn, previewed or saved.
