## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/extrude_and_weave/tour.gif)

1. Extrude sweeps a cross-section along a path, like a moulding round a frame. Select the path (rail) first, then the open cross-section.

   ![Step 1](pictures/extrude_and_weave/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Extrude and Weave**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/extrude_and_weave/step-2.png)

3. Choose how the cross-section follows the rail, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/extrude_and_weave/step-3.png)

4. The quarter-round runs all the way round the rectangle: a picture frame moulding.

   ![Step 4](pictures/extrude_and_weave/step-4.png)
