## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/edit_picture/tour.gif)

1. Make a picture lighter, darker or more contrasty before you trace or carve it. Select the picture.

   ![Step 1](pictures/edit_picture/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Edit Picture**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/edit_picture/step-2.png)

3. Move the sliders for contrast, brightness and gamma; tick Invert to swap light and dark.

   ![Step 3](pictures/edit_picture/step-3.png)
