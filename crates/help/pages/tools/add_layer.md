**Add Layer** makes a new, empty layer at the top of the Layers list and makes it current, so
what you draw or import next goes on it. Layers keep a drawing tidy: put the engraving on one,
the cut-out on another, and show, hide or select each on its own.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/add_layer/tour.gif)

1. Open **Layers** at the top of the Design page and press **Add Layer** under the list.

   ![Step 1](pictures/add_layer/step-1.png)

2. Type a name that says what the layer holds, such as Engraving or Cut Out.

   ![Step 2](pictures/add_layer/step-2.png)

3. Press **Create**. The new layer goes to the top of the list and is made current, so what you draw next goes on it.

   ![Step 3](pictures/add_layer/step-3.png)

## Good to know

- Name a layer for what it holds (Engraving, Pocket, Cut Out). The name shows in the Layers
  list and in the layer box at the top of the view.
- Change a layer's name, colour or material later in [Layer Properties](help:layer_properties).
- Click a layer's name to make it current: see [Set Current Layer](help:set_current_layer).
