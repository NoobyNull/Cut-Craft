## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/pan/tour.gif)

1. Pan slides the view around without changing anything.

   ![Step 1](pictures/pan/step-1.png)

2. Press **Pan** (under 2D View Control).

   ![Step 2](pictures/pan/step-2.png)

3. Drag in the view to slide it. The mouse wheel zooms in and out wherever you are.

   ![Step 3](pictures/pan/step-3.png)
