## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/align_right/tour.gif)

1. Line up the right edges of the selected shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/align_right/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Align Right**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/align_right/step-2.png)

3. Their right edges now line up.

   ![Step 3](pictures/align_right/step-3.png)
