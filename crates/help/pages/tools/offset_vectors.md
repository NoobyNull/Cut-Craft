## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/offset_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Draw a copy of a shape a set distance outside or inside it: a border, a frame or an inlay. Select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/offset_vectors/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Offset Vectors**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/offset_vectors/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Distance** and choose outside or inside, then press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/offset_vectors/step-3.png)
