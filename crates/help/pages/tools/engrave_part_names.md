## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/engrave_part_names/tour.gif)

1. Draw each name as text in the middle of its shape, so a quick engrave marks them all. Name the shapes first.

   ![Step 1](pictures/engrave_part_names/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Engrave Part Names**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/engrave_part_names/step-2.png)

3. The names are drawn on a Part Names layer, ready for a [Quick Engrave](help:quick_engrave_toolpath).

   ![Step 3](pictures/engrave_part_names/step-3.png)
