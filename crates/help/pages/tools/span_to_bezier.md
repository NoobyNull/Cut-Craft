**Convert Span to Bezier** turns the span you right-click into a curve with two handles. Drag the
handles to bend it into any shape. It is on node editing's right-click menu.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/span_to_bezier/tour.gif)

1. Select the shape and press **Node Edit**, then right-click the span to make into a curve.

   ![Step 1](pictures/span_to_bezier/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Convert Span to Bezier**. It gets two handles: drag them to bend the span into any curve.

   ![Step 2](pictures/span_to_bezier/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- A span made into a Bezier starts out straight: it bends only once you drag a handle.
- See [Convert Span to Arc](help:span_to_arc) for a round arc.
