## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_polygon/tour.gif)

1. Draw a shape with equal sides: a triangle, a hexagon, an octagon.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_polygon/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Polygon**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_polygon/step-2.png)

3. Set the number of **Sides**, then drag from the middle out, or type the centre and radius.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_polygon/step-3.png)
