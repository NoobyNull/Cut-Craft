**Close With a Line** joins an open vector's end back to its start with a straight line, so it
becomes a closed shape you can pocket or cut out inside or outside. It is on node editing's right-click menu.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/close_with_line/tour.gif)

1. Select an open vector, then press **Node Edit** on the Design page (under **Edit**).

   ![Step 1](pictures/close_with_line/step-1.png)

2. Right-click on the vector and choose **Close With a Line**. A straight line now joins its end back to its start.

   ![Step 2](pictures/close_with_line/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- To close several open vectors, or join two into one, use
  [Join / Close Vectors](help:join_close_vectors) instead.
- [Select All Open Vectors](help:select_all_open_vectors) finds every open vector in the job.
