## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/clipart_library/tour.gif)

1. The clipart library holds ready-made drawings, pictures and 3D models in your folders.

   ![Step 1](pictures/clipart_library/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Import**, press **Clipart Library**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/clipart_library/step-2.png)

3. The Library opens: drag a piece onto the job.

   ![Step 3](pictures/clipart_library/step-3.png)
