## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/cross_section/tour.gif)

1. A cross-section draws the model's profile along a line, to check it or reuse it as a sweep's profile.

   ![Step 1](pictures/cross_section/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Cross Section Vector**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/cross_section/step-2.png)

3. Drag a line across the model: its profile is drawn as a new line.

   ![Step 3](pictures/cross_section/step-3.png)
