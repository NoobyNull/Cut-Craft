## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/join_straight_line/tour.gif)

1. Join the ends of the selected open lines with straight lines.

   ![Step 1](pictures/join_straight_line/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Join With Straight Line**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/join_straight_line/step-2.png)

3. The nearest ends are joined by straight lines.

   ![Step 3](pictures/join_straight_line/step-3.png)
