## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/tour.gif)

1. Draw a circle from one edge to the other, rather than from its middle.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-1.png)

2. Under **Create**, press **Draw Circle**: one button draws every way.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-2.png)

3. At the top of its form, under **Draw from**, pick **Edge**. Its button opens that way next time.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-3.png)

4. Drag across the circle from edge to edge, or type it in the form.

   ![Step 4](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-4.png)

Pick **Center** under **Draw from** to go back to drawing from the middle out.

Rather have a button of its own for each way? Set **Options ▸ General ▸ Other ways to draw a
shape** to **As buttons** ([Settings](help:settings)), and **Draw Circle from Edge** gets its
own button beside Draw Circle again. Find Tool (**Ctrl+K**) finds it either way.
