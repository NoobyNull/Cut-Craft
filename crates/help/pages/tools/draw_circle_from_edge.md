## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/tour.gif)

1. Draw a circle from one edge to the other, rather than from its middle.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-1.png)

2. Under **Create**, press **Draw Circle From Edge** (it shares a button with Draw Circle).

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-2.png)

3. Drag across the circle from edge to edge, or type it in the form.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_circle_from_edge/step-3.png)
