## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/export_as_stl/tour.gif)

1. Save the 3D model as an STL file, for a 3D printer or another program.

   ![Step 1](pictures/export_as_stl/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Export Model as STL** (under Export Model) and name the file.

   ![Step 2](pictures/export_as_stl/step-2.png)
