To make one model taller or lower, click it in the 3D view and drag one of its height buttons. See [3D View](help:view_3d), Changing a model's height.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/transform/tour.gif)

1. Transform moves, turns and resizes with the handles round the selection. Select the shape.

   ![Step 1](pictures/transform/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Transform**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/transform/step-2.png)

3. Drag a corner handle to resize, the round handle to turn it, or the middle to move it.

   ![Step 3](pictures/transform/step-3.png)

## Which way the handles go

The middle handle is a cross of arrows: the **red** arrows run along **X** (+ to the right)
and the **green** ones along **Y** (+ away from you, up the screen in the 2D view). In the
[3D view](help:view_3d) the cross lies along X and Y on the material, however the view is
turned, so you can see which way a drag will move it. The round handles turn the selection
about **Z**, so they are **blue**, the colour Z has everywhere.

![The move cross with red X and green Y arrows, and the blue turn handles](pictures/transform-axis-handles.png)

To put something back after a drag, use **Undo** (Ctrl+Z): the drag is made when you let go.
Press **Escape** before you let go to cancel the drag with nothing changed. To set an exact
move or turn instead, use [Move](help:move) or [Rotate](help:rotate).
