## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/load_template/tour.gif)

1. A toolpath template brings saved toolpath settings and bits into this job. On the Toolpaths page, press **Load Toolpath Template** (under Templates) and pick the template file.

   ![Step 1](pictures/load_template/step-1.png)

2. Its toolpaths join the list, ready for you to pick the vectors each one cuts: select the shapes, edit the toolpath and press **Calculate**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/load_template/step-2.png)
