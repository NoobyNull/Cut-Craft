**Move to Level** moves the selected 3D components onto another level of the model, which changes
how they combine with the rest. Right-click a component ▸ **Move to Level**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/move_to_level/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** and pick the component to move to another level.

   ![Step 1](pictures/move_to_level/step-1.png)

2. Right-click it and choose **Move to Level**, then the level. It now combines with that level's components.

   ![Step 2](pictures/move_to_level/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Levels combine from the bottom up. See [Insert New Level](help:insert_level).
