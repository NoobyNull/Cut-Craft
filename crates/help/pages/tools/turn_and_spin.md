## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/turn_and_spin/tour.gif)

1. Turn and Spin makes a round 3D shape from a profile line: a lathe shape like a spindle, or a dish spun round one end. Select the open profile.

   ![Step 1](pictures/turn_and_spin/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Turn and Spin**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/turn_and_spin/step-2.png)

3. Choose **Turn** for a lathe shape or **Spin** for a dish or dome, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/turn_and_spin/step-3.png)

4. The profile is turned into a round 3D shape, like a spindle on a lathe.

   ![Step 4](pictures/turn_and_spin/step-4.png)
