**Select All Duplicate Vectors** (Edit ▸ Selection) selects every vector lying exactly on top of
another one. Drawings from other programs often have them, and they would be cut twice. Press
**Delete** to keep one of each.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/select_all_duplicate_vectors/tour.gif)

1. A drawing brought in can have shapes drawn twice, one on top of the other: they would be cut twice.

   ![Step 1](pictures/select_all_duplicate_vectors/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Edit ▸ Selection ▸ Select All Duplicate Vectors**. The copies are selected: press **Delete** to keep one of each.

   ![Step 2](pictures/select_all_duplicate_vectors/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- It only picks from visible, unlocked layers.
