## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/copy_along_vectors/tour.gif)

1. Copy a shape along a line. Select the shape first, then Shift-click the line.

   ![Step 1](pictures/copy_along_vectors/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Arrange**, press **Copy Along Vectors**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/copy_along_vectors/step-2.png)

3. Set how many copies or the gap between them, and whether they turn to follow the line.

   ![Step 3](pictures/copy_along_vectors/step-3.png)
