**Wrapped Spiral Layout** (Gadgets menu) lays out rope or barley-twist strands on a rotary job.
Each strand runs along the cylinder turning as it goes, so wrapped round it is a spiral. It draws
vectors on the unrolled surface for Profile toolpaths.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/wrapped_spiral_layout/tour.gif)

1. In a rotary job, choose **Gadgets ▸ Wrapped Spiral Layout** and set the **Number of strands** twisting round the piece.

   ![Step 1](pictures/wrapped_spiral_layout/step-1.png)

2. Set the **Distance** (with Pitch: how far a strand goes in one turn) and the **Twist**: which way it turns.

   ![Step 2](pictures/wrapped_spiral_layout/step-2.png)

3. Press **Apply**. The strands are laid out on the unrolled surface; wrapped round, they are a spiral.

   ![Step 3](pictures/wrapped_spiral_layout/step-3.png)

## Settings

- **Number of strands**: how many twist round the piece.
- **Start offset** and **End offset**: where the strands start and end.
- **Spacing** and **Distance**: the distance is the **Pitch** (how far a strand goes in one
  turn) or the gap **Between strands**.
- **Twist**: right-hand like a screw thread, or left-hand.
- **Cove at start** and **Cove at end**: a groove round the piece there.
