**Ungroup** on a merged toolpath's right-click menu takes it apart: the merged toolpath goes, and
the toolpaths it was made from are shown again.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/ungroup_merged_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Right-click a merged toolpath (made with **Toolpaths ▸ Merge Visible Toolpaths**) in the list.

   ![Step 1](pictures/ungroup_merged_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Ungroup**. The merged toolpath goes, and the toolpaths it was made from are shown again.

   ![Step 2](pictures/ungroup_merged_toolpath/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Make a merged toolpath with **Toolpaths ▸ Merge Visible Toolpaths**: see
  [Merged Toolpath](help:merged_toolpath).

A merged toolpath holds toolpaths that all use the same bit; ungrouping gives each one back as it was.
