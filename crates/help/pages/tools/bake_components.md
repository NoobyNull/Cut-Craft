## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/bake_components/tour.gif)

1. Bake joins all the visible 3D parts into one.

   ![Step 1](pictures/bake_components/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Bake Components**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/bake_components/step-2.png)

3. The visible parts are replaced by one part holding the same model.

   ![Step 3](pictures/bake_components/step-3.png)
