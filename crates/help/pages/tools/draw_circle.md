## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_circle/tour.gif)

1. On the Design page, press **Draw Circle**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_circle/step-1.png)

2. Click on the sheet where the middle of the circle goes, move out, and click again when it is the size you want.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_circle/step-2.png)

3. To make it an exact size, type the **Radius** (or the centre's X and Y) in the form. The circle on the sheet follows what you type.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_circle/step-3.png)

Next, turn the circle into something the machine cuts: a [Pocket](help:pocket_toolpath) clears its inside, a [Profile](help:profile_toolpath) cuts round its edge.
