## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/prism_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Prism carving leaves each shape standing as a raised, ridged bevel, like a cut gem. Select the shapes.

   ![Step 1](pictures/prism_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Prism Carving** (under Carve and Engrave).

   ![Step 2](pictures/prism_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Prism Depth**: how deep the sloping sides go. Too shallow and the top is left flat; deep enough and they meet in a sharp ridge. **Depth for Full Prism** works that depth out for you.

   ![Step 3](pictures/prism_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Check the bit is a **V-bit**: its sloping sides make the bevel.

   ![Step 4](pictures/prism_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate** to make the toolpath.

   ![Step 5](pictures/prism_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths**: a sloping bevel runs round the star. A wider V-bit, or a smaller shape, brings the slopes together in a ridge.

   ![Step 6](pictures/prism_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).
