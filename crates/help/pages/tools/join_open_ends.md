## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/join_open_ends/tour.gif)

1. Join two open ends by clicking them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/join_open_ends/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Join Open Ends**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/join_open_ends/step-2.png)

3. Click an open end, then the end it should meet. Choose a straight line, running both ends on until they meet, or a curve.

   ![Step 3](pictures/join_open_ends/step-3.png)
