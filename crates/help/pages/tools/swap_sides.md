In a double-sided job, **Swap Sides** turns to the other side of the material. Each side keeps its
own vectors, components, pictures and toolpaths. Choose **Edit ▸ Swap Sides**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/swap_sides/tour.gif)

1. A double-sided job has a top and a bottom side, each with its own vectors, models and toolpaths.

   ![Step 1](pictures/swap_sides/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Edit ▸ Swap Sides** (or click the side's name at the top of the view) to work on the other side.

   ![Step 2](pictures/swap_sides/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- Set a job double-sided, and how the board is turned over, in [Job Setup](help:job_setup).
- Copy vectors to line up on the other side with
  [Copy to Other Side](help:copy_to_other_side).
