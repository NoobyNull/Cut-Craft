## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/sculpt/tour.gif)

1. Sculpting changes a 3D part with a brush, like modelling clay. Select the part in the Components list.

   ![Step 1](pictures/sculpt/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Sculpt** (under Shape Editing).

   ![Step 2](pictures/sculpt/step-2.png)

3. Pick smooth, smudge, add or remove, set the brush size, and drag over the part in the 3D view.

   ![Step 3](pictures/sculpt/step-3.png)
