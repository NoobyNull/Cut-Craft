## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/component_boundary/tour.gif)

1. Draw an outline round the 3D parts, to cut them out or to limit a toolpath. Select the parts, or none for all.

   ![Step 1](pictures/component_boundary/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Vector Boundary Around Selected Components**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/component_boundary/step-2.png)

3. A closed outline follows the edge of the parts.

   ![Step 3](pictures/component_boundary/step-3.png)
