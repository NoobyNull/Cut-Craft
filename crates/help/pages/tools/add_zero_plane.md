## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/add_zero_plane/tour.gif)

1. Add Zero Plane makes a flat base at height 0 over the whole board, for other 3D parts to sit on.

   ![Step 1](pictures/add_zero_plane/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Add Zero Plane**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/add_zero_plane/step-2.png)

3. A flat component covering the material joins the Components list.

   ![Step 3](pictures/add_zero_plane/step-3.png)
