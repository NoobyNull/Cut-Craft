**Space Horizontally** spreads the selected objects so the gaps between them across the job are
even. The leftmost and rightmost stay where they are. It is in **Edit ▸ Align Selected Objects**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/space_horizontally/tour.gif)

1. Select three or more shapes. The outermost two stay where they are.

   ![Step 1](pictures/space_horizontally/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Edit ▸ Align Selected Objects ▸ Left To Right**. The gaps between them are now even across.

   ![Step 2](pictures/space_horizontally/step-2.png)

## The choices

- **Left To Right**: the first one you picked goes on the left.
- **Right To Left**: the first one you picked goes on the right.
- **Horizontally Inside Last Vector**: spreads them inside the last vector picked, which stays
  put.

See [Space Vertically](help:space_vertically) for spacing up the job.
