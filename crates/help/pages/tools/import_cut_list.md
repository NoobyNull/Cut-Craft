## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/import_cut_list/tour.gif)

1. Draw a rectangle for each part in a spreadsheet's cut list (saved as CSV): name, length, width and how many.

   ![Step 1](pictures/import_cut_list/step-1.png)

2. Under **Import**, press **Import Cut List** and pick the file. Then use [Nest Parts](help:nest_parts) to lay them out on the board.

   ![Step 2](pictures/import_cut_list/step-2.png)
