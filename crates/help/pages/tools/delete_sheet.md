**Delete** on a sheet's right-click menu removes that sheet and everything on it. A job always
keeps one sheet. Deleting the active sheet makes the next one active.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete_sheet/tour.gif)

1. Open **Sheets** at the top of the Design page and right-click the sheet to remove.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete_sheet/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Delete**. The sheet goes with everything on it; a job always keeps one sheet.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete_sheet/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- To keep its vectors, move them to another sheet first with
  [Copy / Move to Sheet](help:send_to_sheet).
