## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/import_into_shape/tour.gif)

1. Fit a drawing or picture inside a shape you already have: select the shape first (a circle, plaque or heart).

   ![Step 1](pictures/import_into_shape/step-1.png)

2. Under **Import**, press **Import Into Selected Shape** and pick the file. Choose to scale it, stretch it or bend it to the outline. Select an open line instead to bend the drawing along it.

   ![Step 2](pictures/import_into_shape/step-2.png)
