## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/texture_area/tour.gif)

1. Texture Area tiles a small 3D piece over an area, like scales or studs. Select the piece in the Components list and the area's outline.

   ![Step 1](pictures/texture_area/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Create Texture Area**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/texture_area/step-2.png)

3. Set the spacing and the shifts between rows, then press **Create**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/texture_area/step-3.png)

4. Copies of the piece are tiled across the area inside the shape.

   ![Step 4](pictures/texture_area/step-4.png)
