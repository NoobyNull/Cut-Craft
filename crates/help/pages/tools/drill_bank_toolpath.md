## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/drill_bank_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. A drill bank is a head with several drills side by side, found on some panel machines. Draw a circle for each hole and select them.

   ![Step 1](pictures/drill_bank_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Drill Bank** (under Holes).

   ![Step 2](pictures/drill_bank_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Depth** of the holes. The hole sizes come from the drills themselves.

   ![Step 3](pictures/drill_bank_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. The drills come from **Machine Configuration**. Describe your machine's drill bank there first: each drill's place and size. Then press **Calculate**: each plunge drops the drills that line up with holes and keeps the others up.

   ![Step 4](pictures/drill_bank_toolpath/step-4.png)

No drill bank? Use [Drilling](help:drilling_toolpath): one bit drills the holes one at a time.
