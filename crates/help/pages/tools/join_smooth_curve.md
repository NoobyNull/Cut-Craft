## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/join_smooth_curve/tour.gif)

1. Join the ends of the selected open lines with smooth curves.

   ![Step 1](pictures/join_smooth_curve/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Edit**, press **Join With Smooth Curve**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/join_smooth_curve/step-2.png)

3. The nearest ends are joined by curves that carry on their direction.

   ![Step 3](pictures/join_smooth_curve/step-3.png)
