## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_rectangle/tour.gif)

1. On the Design page, press **Draw Rectangle**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_rectangle/step-1.png)

2. Click on the sheet where one corner goes, move to the opposite corner, and click again.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_rectangle/step-2.png)

3. For an exact size, type the **Width** and **Height** in the form. The rectangle on the sheet follows what you type.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_rectangle/step-3.png)

4. **Corners** can be square, rounded or cut in; **Radius** sets how big the rounding is.

   ![Step 4](pictures/draw_rectangle/step-4.png)

Next, make a toolpath for it: a [Profile](help:profile_toolpath) cuts it out, a [Pocket](help:pocket_toolpath) clears its inside.

**Draw from**, at the top of the form, picks how the drag draws it: **Corner** (from one
corner to the opposite one) or **Center** (out from the middle, the same distance each way; see
[Draw Rectangle from Center](help:draw_rectangle_from_center)). The button opens the way you
picked last.
