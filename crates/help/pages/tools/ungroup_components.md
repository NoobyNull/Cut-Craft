**Ungroup** on a component group's right-click menu takes the group apart. Its components stay
where they are, each on its own again.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/ungroup_components/tour.gif)

1. Open **Components** and right-click a group's name, or a component in it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/ungroup_components/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Ungroup**. Its components are on their own again, where they were.

   ![Step 2](pictures/ungroup_components/step-2.png)
