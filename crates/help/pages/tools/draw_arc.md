## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_arc/tour.gif)

1. Draw part of a circle: an arc.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_arc/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Arc**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_arc/step-2.png)

3. Set the centre, radius, and the start and end angles. It runs anticlockwise from the start to the end.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_arc/step-3.png)
