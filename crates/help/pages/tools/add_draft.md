## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/add_draft/tour.gif)

1. Add Draft makes the model's steep walls slope, so it lifts out of a mould.

   ![Step 1](pictures/add_draft/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Add Draft to Model**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/add_draft/step-2.png)

3. Set the draft angle and press **Apply**.

   ![Step 3](pictures/add_draft/step-3.png)

4. The steep walls now slope outwards, so the part lifts out of a mould.

   ![Step 4](pictures/add_draft/step-4.png)
