**Duplicate** on a toolpath's right-click menu adds a copy of it to the Toolpath List, named
with the next free number, such as "Cut out (1)". Change the copy's settings to try something
new without losing the toolpath you had.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/duplicate_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Right-click a toolpath in the Toolpath List and choose **Duplicate**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/duplicate_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. A copy is added, named with the next number: change its settings to try something new without losing the first.

   ![Step 2](pictures/duplicate_toolpath/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- The copy cuts the same vectors. Untick one of the two so they aren't both saved and cut.
- To use the same toolpath on another sheet, use
  [Copy to Sheet](help:copy_toolpath_to_sheet).
