Double-click a group to step inside it and change one thing in it. See [Groups](help:groups).

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/edit_text/tour.gif)

1. Change words you made with Draw Text: select the text.

   ![Step 1](pictures/edit_text/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Edit Text**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/edit_text/step-2.png)

3. The text's form opens: change the words, font or size, and the letters on the job follow.

   ![Step 3](pictures/edit_text/step-3.png)
