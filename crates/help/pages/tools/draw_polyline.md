## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/draw_polyline/tour.gif)

1. Draw lines through points you click: a zigzag, an outline, a path.

   ![Step 1](pictures/draw_polyline/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Create**, press **Draw Line / Polyline**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/draw_polyline/step-2.png)

3. Click each corner in the view, or drag for a curved span. Click the first point again to close the shape, or press Esc to finish an open line. You can also type the points in the form.

   ![Step 3](pictures/draw_polyline/step-3.png)
