## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. Photo V-carving carves a picture as grooves with a V-bit: deeper, so wider, where it is darker. Import the picture and select it.

   ![Step 1](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Photo V-Carve** (under Carve and Engrave).

   ![Step 2](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Max carving depth**: how deep the grooves go where the picture is black.

   ![Step 3](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Choose the **Strategy**: straight lines across (**Raster**), crossing lines (**Hatch**), or along vectors you drew.

   ![Step 4](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate**. Use a V-bit with a fine point for detail.

   ![Step 5](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths** to see the picture in grooves.

   ![Step 6](pictures/photo_vcarve_toolpath/step-6.png)

When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).

The **Bit** box on the form says which bit cuts it: **Select…** picks another from the [Bit Library](help:tool_database), and **Edit…** changes its sizes, feeds and speeds there.
