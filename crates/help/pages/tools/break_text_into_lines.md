**Break Block into Lines** splits a block of text of several lines into one piece of text for
each line. Each line can then be moved, sized or edited on its own, and stays editable text.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/break_text_into_lines/tour.gif)

1. Select a block of text with more than one line, made with **Draw Text**.

   ![Step 1](pictures/break_text_into_lines/step-1.png)

2. Right-click it and choose **Text ▸ Break Block into Lines**. Each line is now text of its own, to move or edit apart.

   ![Step 2](pictures/break_text_into_lines/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- It is on the right-click menu under **Text**, greyed out unless the selected text has more
  than one line.
- Change the words of a line afterwards with [Edit Text](help:edit_text).
