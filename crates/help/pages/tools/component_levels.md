## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/component_levels/tour.gif)

1. Levels stack 3D parts: each level is combined on its own, then the levels are combined, so one part can sit on another.

   ![Step 1](pictures/component_levels/step-1.png)

2. On the Design page, under **Model**, press **Component Levels**.

   ![Step 2](pictures/component_levels/step-2.png)

3. Add levels and set how each one combines with those under it.

   ![Step 3](pictures/component_levels/step-3.png)
