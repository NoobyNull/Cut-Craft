## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/tour.gif)

1. A chamfer is a sloped edge. Select the shapes whose edges you want sloped.

   ![Step 1](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/step-1.png)

2. On the Toolpaths page, press **Chamfer** (under Edges and Inlays).

   ![Step 2](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/step-2.png)

3. Set the **Width** across the slope. With a V-bit, its angle sets how steep the slope is.

   ![Step 3](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/step-3.png)

4. Choose the **Side**: **Outside** slopes the edge of a part you cut out, **Inside** slopes the rim of a hole or recess.

   ![Step 4](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/step-4.png)

5. Press **Calculate** to make the toolpath.

   ![Step 5](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/step-5.png)

6. Press **Preview All Toolpaths** to see the sloped edge.

   ![Step 6](pictures/chamfer_toolpath/step-6.png)

Cut the part out afterwards with a [Profile](help:profile_toolpath). When it looks right in the preview, [save it for your machine](help:save_toolpaths).

The **Bit** box on the form says which bit cuts it: **Select…** picks another from the [Bit Library](help:tool_database), and **Edit…** changes its sizes, feeds and speeds there.
