**Delete Layer** removes one layer and every vector on it. Right-click the layer in **Layers**.

## Steps

![The steps, one after another](pictures/delete_layer/tour.gif)

1. Open **Layers** at the top of the Design page and right-click the layer to remove.

   ![Step 1](pictures/delete_layer/step-1.png)

2. Choose **Delete Layer**. The layer goes, and every vector on it goes with it.

   ![Step 2](pictures/delete_layer/step-2.png)

## Good to know

- It is greyed out on the last layer: a job always has one.
- To keep the vectors, move them first with [Move to Layer](help:move_to_layer).
- To clear out every empty layer at once, use **Delete ▸ Empty**: see
  [Delete Items](help:delete_items).
