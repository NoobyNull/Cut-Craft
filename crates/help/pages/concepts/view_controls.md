# Moving the views: mouse, trackpad and Invert

The 2D and 3D views move the same way with a mouse or a trackpad. If a direction feels
backwards to you, every control can be turned the other way in **Edit ▸ Options ▸ Mouse
and Shortcuts**.

![Options, Mouse and Shortcuts: Trackpad mode and an Invert box for each control](pictures/options-mouse-and-trackpad.png)

## With a mouse

- **Roll the wheel** to zoom in or out where the pointer is.
- **Tilt the wheel** left or right (or hold **Shift** and roll it) to slide the view sideways.
- **Drag with the wheel pressed** to slide the view.
- In the [3D view](help:view_3d), **drag** with the left or right button to turn the job.

## With a trackpad

- **Pinch** to zoom in or out where the pointer is, in both views.
- With two fingers, the trackpad scrolls like a wheel, so it zooms. To make two fingers
  **slide the view** instead, turn on **Trackpad mode**. Zoom with a pinch then.

## Turning a control the other way

1. Open **Edit ▸ Options** and pick **Mouse and Shortcuts** on the left.
2. Tick the box for the control that feels backwards:
   - **Invert drag left / right (3D view)**: dragging sideways turns the job the other way.
   - **Invert drag up / down (3D view)**: dragging down tilts the top of the job away from
     you instead of towards you.
   - **Invert wheel zoom**: rolling the wheel towards you zooms in instead of out.
   - **Invert pinch zoom**: pinching zooms the other way.
   - **Invert two-finger pan** (Trackpad mode only): the view moves against your fingers
     instead of with them.
   - **Tilting the wheel left or right** has its own choice: pan sideways, pan the other
     way, or do nothing.
3. Press **Apply**. Nothing changes until you do, and **Cancel** puts it back.

A [SpaceMouse](help:settings) has its own Invert boxes on the **SpaceMouse** page.
