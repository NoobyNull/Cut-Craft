# Messages from the Cut Craft Team

Now and then the people who make Cut Craft have news for you, or a question. It shows in a
small window, once, when Cut Craft starts:

- **A message** is a few lines of news. Read it and press **Close**.
- **An opinion poll** has a button for each choice. Press the one you like. It is only to
  see what people think: nothing is decided by it.

![Help ▸ Messages from the Cut Craft Team: a poll with a button for each choice](pictures/team-messages-poll.png)

## Voting in a poll

1. Read the question, then press the button of your choice.
2. Cut Craft says **Thanks! Your vote for … is in.** Often it also shows how the vote is
   going so far, with how many votes each choice has.

   ![After voting: thanks, and how many votes each choice has so far](pictures/team-messages-voted.png)

3. Changed your mind? Press **Change My Vote** and pick another. Each computer has one
   vote, so your new choice replaces the old one.

**Not Now** closes the window without voting. You can vote later from
**Help ▸ Messages from the Cut Craft Team**.

## What is sent

Asking for a message sends nothing about you. A vote sends only your choice, the version of
Cut Craft and a random number made for this copy of Cut Craft, so each computer votes once.
It is the same random number a [crash report](help:bug_reports) uses, and never your name,
your email or anything about your work. The service keeps it only scrambled.

If the computer is offline, or the service doesn't answer, Cut Craft says nothing and
starts as usual. It never waits for a message.

## Seeing a message again

**Help ▸ Messages from the Cut Craft Team** shows the current message or poll whenever you
like, with a tick on the choice you voted for. If there isn't one, it says so.

## Turning them off

Press **Don't show me messages like this** in the window, or turn off
**Edit ▸ Options ▸ Updates ▸ Show messages from the Cut Craft team**, then press **Apply**
(see [Changing settings](help:settings)). Cut Craft then stops asking when it starts. The
Help menu still shows the current message if you ask for it.

Messages are part of Cut Craft 0.6.19, 0.6.20 and 0.6.21 only. From 0.6.22 on, Cut Craft no
longer asks.
