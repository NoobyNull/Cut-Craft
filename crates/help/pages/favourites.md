# Favourites

Use a few tools all the time? Make them **favourites** and show just those, so you are not
hunting through every group for them.

![Right-click a tool's button to add it to your favourites](pictures/basics/favourites.png)

## Adding a tool

1. **Right-click** the tool's button (or its row in the Rail).
2. Pick **Add to Favourites**.

## Showing your favourites

Where they show depends on your window layout (**View ▸ Window Layout**):

- **Classic**: right-click the **Design** or **Toolpaths** side tab and pick **Show
  Favourites**. That page then shows only your favourites. **Show All Tools** brings every
  tool back.
- **Rail**: click the **★** button at the foot of the column of group icons.
- **Ribbon**: open the **Favourites** tab.

## Changing them

- To take a tool off, right-click it and pick **Remove from Favourites**.
- In Classic and Ribbon, right-click a favourite and pick **Move Earlier** or **Move
  Later** to change their order.

Favourites are kept between runs and are the same in every layout. In the Ribbon, a group
holding a favourite is never folded away when the window is narrow. To move whole panels
about, or save an arrangement of your own, use [Interface Layout](help:interface_layout);
see [Changing settings](help:settings) for the three layouts side by side.
