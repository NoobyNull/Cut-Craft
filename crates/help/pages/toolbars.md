# Toolbars of Your Own

Put the tools you reach for together on a **toolbar of your own**: single tools, whole
tool groups, or both, in the order you like. Make as many as you want, say one for signs
and one for boxes, and name each. [Favourites](help:favourites) stay as they are; your
toolbars sit alongside them.

## Making a toolbar

1. **Right-click** any tool's button or row, in any layout.
2. Pick **Add to Toolbar ▸ New Toolbar**. It is called **My Tools** (then My Tools 2, and
   so on) and shows at once.

To add more, right-click another tool and pick **Add to Toolbar ▸** the toolbar's name. A
tool already on that toolbar is ticked.

To put a **whole group** on a toolbar, right-click the group instead: its icon in the Rail
or the Canvas palette, its name under its icons in the Ribbon, or its heading in Classic.
On the toolbar the group is one button that opens its list of tools. It opens when you
point at it, or when you click it if **Options ▸ General ▸ Canvas tool lists open** is set
to On Click.

## Where they show

Every toolbar shows in every [window layout](help:interface_layout):

- **Ribbon** and **Canvas**: a tab of its own after the built-in tabs.
- **Rail**: a button of its own after the **★** button; it opens the toolbar as a list.
- **Classic**: a section of its own at the foot of the **Design** tab.

## Floating over the drawing

Right-click a toolbar's name (its tab, its Rail list's name or its Classic heading) and
pick **Float over the Drawing**. It becomes a small strip over the drawing. Drag it by its
name to anywhere you like; it stays there next time. Right-click its name and pick **Float
over the Drawing** again to put it back.

## Changing a toolbar

- **Drag** an item along its toolbar to move it; a line shows where it will go.
- Or right-click an item and pick **Move Earlier** or **Move Later**.
- **Remove from Toolbar** takes an item off.
- Right-click the toolbar's name to **Rename Toolbar** or **Delete Toolbar**.

Your toolbars are kept with your other settings, the same in every layout.
