# Finding help

PowerWhittle explains itself as you go. There are five ways to find out what something does,
from a quick note under the pointer to a full page with pictures.

## A short note: point at it

![Point at a setting's name for a short note about it](pictures/basics/hover_note.png)

- **A setting**: point at its name in a form, and a short note says what it does.
- **A tool's button**: point at it to see what it does and the next step.
- **A greyed-out menu item or tool**: point at it to see what it is waiting for, such as a
  shape picked, a toolpath picked in the list, or a job open. Do that, and it is ready.

## The tool's own page: F1

![F1 with a tool's form open opens that tool's help](pictures/basics/f1_help.png)

With a tool's form open, press **F1**. Help opens on that tool's page: what it does, the
steps, pictures and every setting. With no form open, F1 opens **Help Contents**.

## Search help

Along the top of the Help window, **Search help** answers a question the way you'd ask it:
"how do I cut out a sign with tabs?", or just a word such as "stepover". The pages that
answer it best come first.

![A question typed in the search box, and the pages that answer it, best first](pictures/help/ask-a-question.png)

It knows words that mean the same (bridges and hold-downs find tabs, "cut out" finds
Profile) and other endings of a word ("pockets" finds Pocket), so you don't need PowerWhittle's
own name for a thing.

## Finding a tool by how it looks

- Each tool's help page shows the picture on its button beside its name, and search
  results show it too, so you can tell you have the right tool.
- **?** at the top of the Design or Toolpaths page lists that page's tools, each beside the
  picture on its button: handy when you remember the icon but not the name.
- Know the name, or part of it? [Find Tool](help:find_tool) (**Ctrl+K**) opens it.

## Getting about in Help

- **The side bar** beside every page lists **Other tools like this** (the tools in the same
  group, each with its icon), **Related** pages, and **What links here**: the pages that
  point to this one. Click one to go there.

  ![Pocket's help page, with other toolpaths like it, related pages and what links here down its right](pictures/help/side-bar.png)
- **Links in the words**: wherever a page names another tool or idea, the name is a link.
- **◀ Back** and **Forward ▶** go through the pages you have read, like a web browser. A
  mouse's own back and forward buttons and **Alt+Left** / **Alt+Right** do the same.

## Did You Know?

Each time PowerWhittle starts it shows one short tip, and **Tell Me More…** opens the page
that explains it. **Help ▸ Did You Know?** shows them all, one after another, and
[Did You Know?](help:tips) lists every tip by subject. Turn the tip at startup off in
**Edit ▸ Options ▸ General Settings**.

## Credits and the licence

PowerWhittle is built with free libraries, pictures and icons made by other people.
**Help ▸ Credits** (or **Credits…** in **Help ▸ About PowerWhittle**) thanks them and shows each
one's licence in full. Type part of a name, such as *serde*, or a licence, such as *MIT*, in
the search box to find one, and click a line to read its licence. The same credits are in the
`credits` folder that comes with every download.

**Help ▸ About PowerWhittle** sums up PowerWhittle's own licence: people and one-person businesses
may use it for anything, including making and selling things with it, but nobody may share,
sell or change PowerWhittle itself. **Read the full license** there opens the exact terms.
