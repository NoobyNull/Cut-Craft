# Rail layout

A narrow column of tool groups down the left leaves the most room for your drawing. Click a
group to list its tools by name, so you never have to guess an icon.

![The Rail layout, light theme on the left and dark on the right](pictures/layouts/rail-light-and-dark.png)

## Finding a tool

1. Point at an icon in the column on the left to see the group's name.
2. Click it. The group's tools are listed beside the column, each with its icon and name.
3. Click a tool. Its settings open on the right, and the list closes. Press the pin at the
   top of the list to keep it open.

Click the same group icon again to close its list.

## The panels

- **Right**: the open tool's settings. With no tool open, it shows tabs for Sheets, Layers,
  Components, Library, Toolpaths and Checks. **Checks** shows what would stop or spoil the
  cut before you save it.

## Your tools at hand

Right-click a tool in a list and pick **Add to Favourites**. Your favourites float over the
drawing as a bar of their own; right-click its dots and pick **Attach to Rail** to list them
under the **★** button under the groups instead. Once you install a gadget or add-on, its button comes last.
See [Favourites](help:favourites).

To pick another layout, press [Interface Layout](help:interface_layout).
