# Classic layout

Two pages beside the view: the **Design** page of tool icons on the left, the **Toolpaths**
page on the right, and tabs down each side for the rest.

![The Classic layout, light theme on the left and dark on the right](pictures/layouts/classic-light-and-dark.png)

## Finding a tool

1. On the [Design page](help:design), click a sub-tab: **Create**, **Edit**, **Arrange**,
   **Model** or **Import**. File Operations and 2D View Control stay above them.
2. Click a tool's icon. Its settings open in the page.
3. When the drawing is ready, open the [Toolpaths page](help:toolpaths) on the right and
   pick how to cut.

## The tabs at the sides

- **Left**: Design, Sheets, Layers, Components and Library.
- **Right**: Toolpaths.

A pin at the top of each page keeps it open beside the view. The **Switch Layout** button
next to the pin swaps between the arrangement for drawing and the one for making
toolpaths. **F11** and **F12** do the same.

## Your tools at hand

Right-click a tool and pick **Add to Favourites**. Right-click the **Design** or
**Toolpaths** side tab and pick **Show Favourites** to see only those. See
[Favourites](help:favourites).

To pick another layout, press [Interface Layout](help:interface_layout).
